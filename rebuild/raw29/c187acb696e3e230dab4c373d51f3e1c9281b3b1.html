Overview of Entries in Drop-down Lists

In the festo.cfg configuration file, entries from drop-down lists are specified as numbers. In the EPLAN parts management, the values specified below are possible in the different drop-down lists.

Record type

The record types are displayed in the tree view of the Parts management dialog.

Generic product group

This drop-down list is displayed in the General tab of the parts management.

Product group

This drop-down list is displayed in the General tab of the parts management.

Product subgroup

This drop-down list is displayed in the General tab of the parts management.

Mounting surface

This drop-down list is displayed in the Mounting data tab of the parts management.
