Graphical Reports

Graphical reports are clear representations that provide a structured overview of the process of a plant, a machine, or a device. In EPLAN, you can, for example, create simple text-based functional diagrams with placed property texts and symbol-based functional diagrams with inserted device symbols.

Graphical reports may occur multiple times in the project.
