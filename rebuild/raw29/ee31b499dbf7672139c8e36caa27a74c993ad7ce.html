ECAD

The ECAD discipline serves to create one of the schematic fragments consisting of page and window macros. The schematic fragments are allocated to the mechatronical components of the modular system. A discipline-specific concept is defined by means of the Plug-Socket concept.

An ECAD discipline structure is created from the configuration by means of a command. From the resulting ECAD structure the schematics are created, also by means of a command.
