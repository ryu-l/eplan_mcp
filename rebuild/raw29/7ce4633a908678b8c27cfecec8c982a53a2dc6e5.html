Copper Rail: Changing the Length

Each side of a placed copper item can be shortened or extended subsequently. If a delivery length is entered on the rail part used, this will be taken into account in case of an extension, and the length change is possible only up to this value.

Preconditions:

  • You have opened a project. A layout space is open.
  • The layout space contains placed busbars (bending).
  1. Edit > Graphic > Change length
  2. To modify the length of a leg on a bent copper rail, move the cursor over the item.

    Positions where the length can be modified are marked with arrows.
  3. Select the green arrow at the position you wish to extend / shorten.
  4. Move the end point of the selected leg, or enter a positive or negative length value on the keyboard.
