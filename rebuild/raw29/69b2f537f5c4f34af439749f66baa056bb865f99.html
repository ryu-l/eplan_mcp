Editing Legend Items

In the case of changes to the equipping of a mounting panel it may become necessary to renumber the legend items.

Preconditions:

  • You have opened a project.
  • You have opened the 2D panel layout - <Project name> dialog.
  • In the Tree tab you have selected the mounting panel whose legend items are to be updated. (Multiple selection isn't possible here.)
  1. Select Popup menu > Edit legend item.
  2. In the Edit legend item dialog make use of the buttons , , , and to move the legend items upwards or downwards or to the beginning / end of the list.
  3. Click [Number].
  4. In the Number dialog, define the numbering settings.
  5. Click [OK].

    The entries in the Legend item column are adjusted.
  6. Click [OK].

    The legend items are updated.

The updated entries can be displayed in the List tab (Popup menu > Configure columns in the list and selection of the Legend item check box).
