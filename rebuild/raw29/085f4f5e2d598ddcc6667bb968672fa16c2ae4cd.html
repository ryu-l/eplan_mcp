Customer-focused Improvement Program

The EPLAN Software & Service team works continuously to improve the EPLAN software in order to live up to the growing demands on EPLAN products. To implement such improvements in a targeted fashion, it is required to have a good understanding of the users' working method.

How does the customer-focused improvement program of EPLAN work, and how can you benefit from participating?

The improvement program for the EPLAN products is activated by default. In the process no personal data are acquired, but only anonymized information about how you use EPLAN and how you configure your software and hardware. The data are sent to the address eplanux.de using the https protocol, and are subsequently evaluated at EPLAN headquarters in Monheim am Rhein, Germany. EPLAN will use insights gained from such information to further improve the user-friendliness and performance of the software in future versions.

How can you terminate your participation in the customer-focused improvement program?

You can terminate your participation in the improvement program by activating the No, don't participate option in the Customer-focused improvement program dialog. This dialog can be accessed from the main menu via the menu items Help > Customer-focused improvement program.

You can agree to participate at any time again by activating the Yes, participate in the customer-focused improvement program option. Your consent applies only to the started EPLAN product in the current EPLAN version.

Which data are not collected?

Personal data, customer-specific project data or other potentially confidential information are not collected:

  • Name of company, user, computer, IP address, etc. are not collected.
  • Contact data, such as e-mail, phone numbers, addresses, etc. are not collected.
  • Names of projects, pages, files, directory paths, etc. are not collected.
  • Customer, part, macro data, etc. are not collected.
  • Text input is not collected.
  • Your IP address is neither tracked nor stored by EPLAN.

How often are data sent?

After you exit EPLAN, the collected information of the current EPLAN session will be sent.

Does the program affect my work?

Your work will not be interrupted by additional prompts or messages. If your computer is not connected to the Internet, the collected data will be discarded. The performance of your computer will not be affected.

How is the data protected?

The logging function cannot be manipulated externally; external access to the collected data is not possible.

The collected data cannot be assigned to or associated with the creator of the data by EPLAN Software & Service.

The transmitted data are handled confidentially by EPLAN, and are used exclusively for the stated purposes. Any other use is excluded.

The transmitted data are subject to the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany.
