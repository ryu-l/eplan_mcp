Importing Parts from the Festo Product Catalog

Parts from the Festo product catalog can be imported into the EPLAN parts management and then used in a project.

Preconditions:

  • You have opened a project.
  • You have installed the Festo product catalog and have created at least one shopping basket in the order management of the Festo product catalog.
  • In the festo.cfg configuration file, you have defined the desired settings for the import.
  1. Utilities > Parts > Management > [Extras] > Import Festo shopping basket
  2. Select a shopping basket in the order management of the Festo product catalog and click [OK].

    All parts in the shopping basket are transferred to the parts management.

The following information is transferred on import:

  • The part number that uniquely identifies the item. The part number can be constructed from different fields in the Festo product catalog.
  • The assignment of the item to a macro or symbol (if present). The festo.cfg configuration file is used to assign the correct macro in EPLAN.
  • Sales data (e.g., manufacturer, packaging units, price, etc.) from the Festo product catalog. The import can be configured using the festo.cfg configuration file, i.e., you can (e.g.) decide whether you wish to transfer the prices, and whether the existing prices should be overwritten.
  • Hyperlinks on the Festo website where current documentation and technical data of the part are stored.

Import parts from the Festo product catalog into the device list

The device list can be expanded with parts from the Festo product catalog.

Preconditions:

  • You have opened a project.
  • You have installed the Festo product catalog and have created at least one shopping basket in the order management of the Festo product catalog.
  1. Project data > Parts / Devices > Device list
  2. Select the Festo shopping basket popup menu item.
  3. Select a shopping basket in the order management of the Festo product catalog and click [OK].

    All parts in the shopping basket are transferred to the device list.
