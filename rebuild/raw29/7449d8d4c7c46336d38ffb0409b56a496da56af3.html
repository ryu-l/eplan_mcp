Dialog Convert currencies

Dialog call:

Utilities > Parts > Management. You have opened a parts database in the Parts management dialog using [Extras] > Settings and determined both currencies for your price listings with the Options > Settings > User > Management > Parts menu items. Utilities > Parts > Management > [Extras] > Currencies.

In this dialog, you can convert prices for selected parts into different currencies.

The following dialog elements are available:

Currencies group box:

Here the currencies are displayed, along with the conversion factor you specified.

Convert group box:

Here, you can specify the direction in which you want to convert.

Selection group box:

If a price should not be converted (e.g., the sales price) then deselect the corresponding check box in this group box.

The prices of the selected parts will be recalculated according to the currency conversions specified in the Settings.

Note:

The value is rounded to 2 digits after the decimal point.
