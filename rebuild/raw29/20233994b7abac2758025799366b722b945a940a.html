Editing Text Properties

Precondition:

You have opened a page, form, plot frame or symbol.

  1. Double-click a text.
  2. In the Properties (text) dialog, edit the text and its properties. Use the Textand Format tabs, as described under "Inserting texts".
  3. Click [OK].

    The changes are adopted and are immediately visible on the page.
