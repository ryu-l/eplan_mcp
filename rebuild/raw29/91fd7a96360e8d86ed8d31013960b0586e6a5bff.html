Exclusion of liability "Migrating Rittal TS8 Parts to Rittal VX25 Parts"

This program functionality is a conversion aid to migrate part placements of the Rittal baying system TS8 in existing EPLAN Pro Panel projects to the Rittal baying system VX25.

Migration of the Rittal TS system parts is effected on the basis of a migration table that is provided by the manufacturer Rittal.

The migration is effected by part. With regard to the Rittal baying system VX25, a correctly migrated part can be incompatible to the existing mounting situation or the design purpose of its usage. Every migration requires concrete verification by the customer.

The VX25 parts required in accordance with the Rittal migration table are made available via the EPLAN Data Portal and are continuously updated and supplemented by the manufacturer Rittal. Rittal reserves the right to carry out modifications to parts and to the migration table at any time without prior notice.

The program functions provided in EPLAN Pro Panel do not represent customer-specific solutions in this form, but rather solely provide help in the typical tasks involved in the migration of Rittal TS8-based 3D mounting layouts in EPLAN projects.

As the EPLAN customer you are responsible yourself for the proper design and configuration and the documentation of the migrated 3D mounting layouts.

These program functions do not replace a sound knowledge of the handling and correct usage of Rittal system components and do not relieve you of the responsibility for proper and correct handling of Rittal system components in the planning and application for the virtual mounting layout in EPLAN Pro Panel, in the documentation, and in manufacturing.
