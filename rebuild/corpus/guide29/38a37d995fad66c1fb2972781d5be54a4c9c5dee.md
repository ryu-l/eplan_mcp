# Devices

Devices in EPLAN are divided into "general" [devices](Glossary_o_betriebsmittel.htm) (including motors, fuses, valves, etc.) and "special" devices (including terminals, cables, [PLC connection points](Glossary_o_sps_anschluesse.htm), [black boxes](Glossary_o_geraetekaesten.htm), etc.).

The general devices or components in EPLAN have the following characteristics:

- They are inserted using symbol selection.
- EPLAN suggests a DT, which can easily be changed at any time.
- They can be labeled, and the label can be formatted individually.
- They [generate](Glossary_o_erzeugen.htm) automatic [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) ([autoconnecting](Glossary_o_autoconnecting.htm)).
- They are listed in the [reports](Glossary_o_auswertungen.htm).
- The logic of the underlying function can be changed so that the device behaves differently (e.g., with respect to the display of [cross-references](Glossary_o_querverweise.htm)).
- Cross-references are detected automatically and can be displayed.
- A device protection can be assigned to [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm), connections and devices. The device protection applies in particular to those parts that were assigned to a device.

For the special devices, moreover, there are special editing and reporting options.
