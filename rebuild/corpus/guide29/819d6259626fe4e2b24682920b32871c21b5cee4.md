# Tab Doors

[![Closed](../../Skins/Default/Stylesheets/Images/transparent.gif)Dialog call:](javascript:void(0);)

Utilities > Parts > Management. You have selected a [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) in the tree or list view, from the Generic product group "Mechanics", Product group "Enclosure".

In this tab, you can enter the housing-specific data for parts in the Generic product group "Mechanics", Product group "Enclosure". The data can be specified more than once per part, namely once per variant.
