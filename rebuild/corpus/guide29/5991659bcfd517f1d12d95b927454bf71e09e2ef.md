# Enclosure legends: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_schaltschranklegenden.htm)

### Enclosure legends

Electrical engineering

Item-related report, i.e., the DT of the mounting panel must be unique. Shows the relationship between the parts in the schematics, the part position in the bill of materials, and the graphical object or part placement on the mounting panel. All part placements are allocated a sequential number. The enclosure legends can be created as report pages or inserted as "Free placements" into a page of type "Panel layout".
