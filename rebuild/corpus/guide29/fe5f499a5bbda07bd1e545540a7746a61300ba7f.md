# PLC Documentation in Single-line Representation

To simplify the documentation of [wiring](Glossary_o_verdrahtungen.htm) and cabling for programmable logic controllers (PLCs) in plants and machines, the single-line representation is often used. If bus systems are used, the single-line schematic is the ideal page type for visualizing [network structures](Glossary_o_netzwerkstrukturen.htm).

A PLC connection point in single-line representation always represents a signal terminal. In contrast to multi-line representation, in the single-line representation of [PLC connection points](Glossary_o_sps_anschluesse.htm), the power [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) are often not documented. The DT and the connection point number are important for the identification of a PLC connection point, on both multi-line and single-line schematic pages.

To represent network structures a special type of connection point is available - the bus port. The [bus ports](Glossary_o_sps_busanschluesse.htm) connect, for example, a master with several slaves. The connections are shown as single-line cables.
