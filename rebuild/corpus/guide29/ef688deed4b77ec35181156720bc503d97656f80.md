# Introduction to EPLAN Fluid

EPLAN Fluid fulfills your need for more effectiveness with the greatest level of engineering in the [fluid power](Glossary_o_fluidtechnik.htm) sector. EPLAN Fluid is available in two program [variants](Glossary_o_varianten.htm):

- As an independent program
- as a license-based add-on to EPLAN Electric P8 and EPLAN Pro Panel.

What does EPLAN Fluid offer?

- Symbol libraries for the fluid power [trades](Glossary_o_gewerke.htm) of [hydraulics](Glossary_o_hydraulik.htm), [pneumatics](Glossary_o_pneumatik.htm), [cooling](Glossary_o_kuehlung.htm), [lubrication](Glossary_o_schmierung.htm), [cooling lubricant](Glossary_o_kuehlschmiermittel.htm), and [gas engineering](Glossary_o_gastechnik.htm) can be used separately or jointly to graphically display [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) in the project.
- To display junctions and changes of direction, simple [connection symbols](Glossary_o_verbindungssymbole.htm) or [connection splicers](Glossary_o_leitungsverteiler.htm) and [line connectors](Glossary_o_leitungsverbinder.htm) can be placed in the schematic. Connection splicers and line connectors represent definite [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm) that can be evaluated and output in [bills of materials](Glossary_o_stuecklisten.htm) or order lists.
- Fluid [devices](Glossary_o_betriebsmittel.htm) can be identified in compliance with DIN ISO 1219-2. It is also possible to choose from other predefined identification structures.
- According to the standard DIN ISO 1219-2, fluid [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) (pipes and tubes) can be additionally identified by an [ID](Glossary_o_id.htm) code which consists of a line number (optional) and a technical information (mandatory). As an option, in the schematic the tube's nominal size can be specified for tubes, and the nominal external diameter and the wall thickness for pipes or, alternatively, external and internal diameter.
- Tabular editing of function data makes it possible to rename multiple fluid devices in one operation quickly and easily without having to open schematic pages.
- Complex fluid power devices can be implemented with the help of [black boxes](Glossary_o_geraetekaesten.htm) or device groups.
- Reports such as bills of materials, order lists, tubing or piping lists as well as conduit / line plans can be generated and included in the project as [graphical pages](Glossary_o_grafikseiten.htm) at any time.
- Automatic [check runs](Glossary_o_prueflaeufe.htm) provide security when planning and help to [create](Glossary_o_erstellen.htm) error-free schematics and [reports](Glossary_o_auswertungen.htm).
