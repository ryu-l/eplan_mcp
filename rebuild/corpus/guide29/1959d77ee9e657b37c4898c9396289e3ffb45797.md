### Wire harnesses

Electrical engineering

A wire harness is an arrangement of wires and cables and further components (terminals, pins, etc.). The cables are bundled in a cable bundle, for example by clips, cable ties, etc. and are combined into a wire harness and clamped together on a special workbench or on a nailboard.
