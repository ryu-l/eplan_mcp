# Assigning Manual Change Protection

You can use the Configure protection dialog to assign a manual change protection specifically to one or more pages, [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) or [segments](Glossary_o_segmente.htm).

Preconditions:

- You have opened a project.
- You have selected a function in the graphical editor or in a navigator dialog, or you have selected one or more pages in the page navigator. Or you have selected a segment in the preplanning navigator.
- No automatic change protection is activated for the selected [objects](Glossary_o_objekte.htm).

1. Select the Edit > Other > Configure protection menu [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm).
2. In the Configure protection dialog, activate the Manual protection check box.
3. Click [OK].  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) Change protection ([ID](Glossary_o_id.htm) 3014) and Change protection (hierarchical) (ID 3015) are assigned to the objects.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) All the properties of the protected objects can no longer be changed.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The protected objects can no longer be deleted from the project.

### Cancelling the manual change protection

1. Select the desired objects in a navigator dialog or in the graphical editor.
2. Select the Edit > Other > Configure protection menu items.
3. In the Configure protection dialog, deactivate the Manual protection check box.
4. Click [OK].  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The change protection is canceled, meaning that the Change protection and Change protection (hierarchical) properties are deactivated for all the marked objects.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/info.png)Tip:

If you have selected a hierarchy level in a navigator dialog, you can activate or deactivate the change protection for all the objects below this hierarchy level simultaneously.
