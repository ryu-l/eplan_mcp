# Bundle: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_buendel.htm)

### Bundle

User interface / operation

In general practice, tubes, cables and individual connections are often combined by means of cable ties / tubing ties or spiral coiled tubes into a bundle, or are protected against external effects by means of tubing protection. In EPLAN Pro Panel, a bundle is a pre-sorting of routed wires prior to fabrication. Wires to be routed one by one or in parallel are combined in an organizational manner, and provided as labeled bundles for wiring purposes.
