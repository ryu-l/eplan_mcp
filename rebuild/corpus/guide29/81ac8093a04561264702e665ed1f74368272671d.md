# Conduits: Definition of Term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_pakete.htm)

### Conduits

Fluid power

In EPLAN a conduit in fluid power is the combination of several conduit connections (see [Conduit connections](Glossary_o_paketverbindungen.htm)). These conduit connections can be tubes, but also other connections. Mostly they are conduits. Bundles and conduit connections are managed in the same way as cables and cable connections.
