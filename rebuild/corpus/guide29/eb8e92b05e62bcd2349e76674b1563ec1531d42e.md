# P026014: Mounting clearances are not observed

### Cause

When [placing](Glossary_o_auflegen.htm) or moving a [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) on the mounting surface or mounting rail, the mounting clearance from the adjacent part was not observed.

The mounting clearances for a part can be specified in the Parts management, Mounting data tab. Use the Mounting clearance width, Mounting clearance height and Mounting clearance depth fields.

### Solution

1. From the Message management dialog, double click on the message to locate the part in the [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) in question.
2. Move the part on the mounting surface or mounting rail, then start a new check run.
