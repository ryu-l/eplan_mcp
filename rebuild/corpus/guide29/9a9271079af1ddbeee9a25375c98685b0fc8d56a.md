# Graphical Reports

Graphical [reports](Glossary_o_auswertungen.htm) are clear representations that provide a structured overview of the process of a plant, a machine, or a device. In EPLAN, you can, for example, [create](Glossary_o_erstellen.htm) simple text-based [functional diagrams](Glossary_o_funktionsdiagramme.htm) with placed [property texts](Glossary_o_eigenschaftstexte.htm) and symbol-based functional diagrams with inserted device [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm).

Graphical reports may occur multiple times in the project.
