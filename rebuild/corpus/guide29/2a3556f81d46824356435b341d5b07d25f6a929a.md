### Connection point types

Electrical engineering

The connection point type specifies the type of connection (see [Connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm)) allowed at a connection point. The connection point types correspond to the function definitions of the connections, e.g., conductor / wire, jumper, optical fiber, tube, pipe, etc.
