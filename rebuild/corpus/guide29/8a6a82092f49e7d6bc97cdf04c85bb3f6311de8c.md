### Interruption point cross-references

Electrical engineering

Interruption points are used to represent connections encompassing more than one page. Cross-references between interruption points are automatically created by EPLAN, where the cross-reference represents the page reference in the schematic. Two variants of interruption point cross-references can be defined - the star and the chain cross reference.
