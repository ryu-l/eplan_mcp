# Changing Pipings

The course of a freely routed piping can be influenced graphically or through entries in the input box. Graphical changing of a piping is possible parallel to the X, Y and Z axis between the source and target. The piping [route](Glossary_o_verlegen.htm) arising through the free routing can be optimized with as few further editing steps as possible in order to obtain the final piping routing. When editing a piping the design mode can also be used together with the activated object snap.

The following is possible:

- Selection of a pipe segment in the level of the adjacent [segments](Glossary_o_segmente.htm)
- Moving of the selected pipe segment of a freely routed piping
- Orthogonal and non-orthogonal moving of bending positions / piping elbows
- Changing the bending radius of bending positions / piping elbows
- Inserting and deleting additional segments / piping elbows.

Preconditions:

- You have opened a project. A [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) is open.
- The layout space contains freely routed [pipings](Glossary_o_rohrleitungen.htm).

### Moving a pipe segment / bending position

The working method for moving segments and bending positions is the same. The right-angled moving of a pipe segment is described here as an example.

1. Select the following menu [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm): Edit > Graphic > Change bending positions
2. To change the position of a pipe segment at a piping move the cursor over the segment.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The segment is highlighted in color.
3. Click the segment.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) Arrows are displayed in 4 orthogonal directions at the center of the segment.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Visualisation/ALL/piping_movesegment1_as.png)
4. Select the direction in which you want to move the segment by clicking the green arrow.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The segment can be moved in the selected arrow direction.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Visualisation/ALL/piping_movesegment2_as.png)
5. Click the desired end point for the move or enter a distance value in the input box.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The pipe segment / bending position is moved to the selected position. The adjacent segments are adapted correspondingly.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Visualisation/ALL/piping_movesegment3_as.png)
