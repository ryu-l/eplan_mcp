# P026099: Unequal power dissipation layout in the air-conditioning field <x> (Power dissipation center: <y>)

### Cause

The power dissipation layout in the air-conditioning field <x> of an enclosure (enclosure interior) is uneven due to the location of the [part placements](Glossary_o_artikelplatzierungen.htm). The power dissipation center lies either in the top half or the bottom half <y> of the air-conditioning field.

### Solution

1. Locate the corresponding enclosure in the [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) using the Go to (graphic) functionality from the popup menu in the Message management dialog.
2. Move the [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) displacements in the enclosure so that the power dissipation is as even as possible in the complete air-conditioning field.
3. Then start a new check run.
