# Operational Sequence Sheets

Operational sequence sheets are simple graphic representations that provide a structured overview of the process of a plant, a machine or a device. Below are some of the options EPLAN provides you with for editing [operational sequence sheets](Glossary_o_ablaufdiagramme.htm):

- You can [create](Glossary_o_erstellen.htm) [functional diagrams](Glossary_o_funktionsdiagramme.htm), that is, simple path-time or path-step diagrams, according to the VDI 3260 guideline. In this case, you can create both simple text-based functional diagrams with placed [property texts](Glossary_o_eigenschaftstexte.htm) and symbol-based functional diagrams with inserted device [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm).
- You can create [GRAFCET](Glossary_o_grafcet.htm) diagrams. These diagrams are representations of sequential controls for which the specification language GRAFCET (GRAphe Fonctionnel de Commande Etapes/Transitions) according to DIN EN 60848 has been used.
- In creating operational sequence sheets you will be assisted by prefabricated [forms](Glossary_o_formulare.htm) and symbols.
- For [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) and [macros](Glossary_o_makros.htm) to be placed in operational sequence sheets, the "Functional" representation type is available.
