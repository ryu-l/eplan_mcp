# Details on the Use of Terminals in the Single-line Representation

For terminals with single-line representation, it is normal to assign either a terminal designation or just a terminal strip designation.

EPLAN makes both methods of designation available when you insert a single-line terminal symbol.

You cannot store connection or [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) data at terminals with single-line representation. In accordance with this, the Connections and Parts tabs in the Properties <...> dialog are not displayed.

In contrast to this, you can place [terminal strip definitions](Glossary_o_klemmenleistendefinitionen.htm) on single-line schematic pages, to which you can then assign parts.
