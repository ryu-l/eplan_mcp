# P005056: Conflict between net and potential or signal

### Cause

The [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) of a net definition point differ from the properties of the associated potential or signal definition (of the same name) defined in the schematic The potential type, potential value or the frequency information could be different.

### Solution

Enter the corresponding properties for the net definition point and for the potential or signal definition in the schematic.

Refresh the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) and then start a new check run.
