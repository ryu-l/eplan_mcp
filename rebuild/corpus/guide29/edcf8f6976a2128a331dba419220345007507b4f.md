# Plot Frame Exchange

Plot frames define the page size and page alignment of the schematic pages. They also determine the title block, the [grid](Glossary_o_raster.htm), and the breakdown of the schematic page in the path and positioning areas (columns and rows).

When carrying out a change of standards, you can adjust the project pages to a different plot frame. The project pages can thus be displayed in portrait and landscape format.
