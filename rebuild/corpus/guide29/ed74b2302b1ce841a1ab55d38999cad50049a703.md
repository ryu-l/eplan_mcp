# Conductor termination processing: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_aderendbehandlung.htm)

### Conductor termination processing

Mechanics

Assignment of wire termination processing (see [Wire termination processing](Glossary_o_verbindungsende_behandlung.htm)) to the actual equipment of wire ends by the fabrication machine (stripping, crimping, sleeves). For this purpose, you enter the instructions (machine commands) in the language of the respective machine software that are to be executed for the selected conductor termination processing.
