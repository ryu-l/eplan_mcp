# Toolbar Actions

Pre-defined and [user-defined](Glossary_o_benutzerdefiniert.htm) EPLAN [toolbars](Glossary_o_symbolleisten.htm) can also be extended with extra buttons linked to [actions](Glossary_o_aktionen.htm) or menu [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm). This allows you to assign frequently used [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm), [macros](Glossary_o_makros.htm) or other important program actions to toolbars and thus improve the efficiency of the design process.
