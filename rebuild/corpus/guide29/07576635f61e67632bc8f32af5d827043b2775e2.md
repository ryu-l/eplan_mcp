### Functional diagrams

User interface / operation

Operational sequence sheets are simple path-time or path-step diagrams that provide a structured overview of the process of a plant, a machine or a device. In EPLAN, you can create both simple text-based functional diagrams with placed property texts and symbol-based functional diagrams with inserted device symbols.
