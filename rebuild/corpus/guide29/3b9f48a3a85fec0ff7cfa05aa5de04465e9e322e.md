# P002004: Plug without pins

### Cause

You created a plug without [pins](Glossary_o_steckerkontakte.htm) in the Plug Navigator or you placed a plug without pins in the schematic.

### Solution

Create at least one pin in the Plug Navigator via Popup menu > New, or place at least one pin in the schematic via Insert > Symbol. Assign the DT plug definition to the pin and give it a designation.

Refresh the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) and then start a new check run.
