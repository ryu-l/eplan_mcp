# Breaking Down Assemblies and Modules in the Enclosure Legends

Before outputting [enclosure legends](Glossary_o_schaltschranklegenden.htm) you can now specify in the [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) that the [assemblies](Glossary_o_baugruppen.htm) and [modules](Glossary_o_module.htm) in the enclosure are to be broken down in their individual parts.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/benefit.png)Benefit:

Thanks to the extension in the settings for the [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) assemblies you can list all components of an assembly or a module in an enclosure legend.

To this purpose select the Part menu item in the Reports - <Project name> dialog under the [Settings] button. In the following dialog Settings: Parts the settings in the group box Part assemblies now also have an effect on the enclosure legend. So that the individual parts of assemblies and modules are listed separately, activate the check boxes Break up assemblies and Break up modules.
