# Shading: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_shading.htm)

### Shading

Mechanics

Shading is a term used in 3D computer graphics to describe the simulation of the surface of an object, for example by the display of color-filled areas. In EPLAN 3D modules the enclosure and its items are represented by color-filled areas.
