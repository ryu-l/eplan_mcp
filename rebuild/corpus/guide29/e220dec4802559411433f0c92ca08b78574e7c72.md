# Form-based Contact Image

EPLAN offers the possibility of individually forming tabular contact images at contactors and motor overload switches in schematics on the basis of embedded [forms](Glossary_o_formulare.htm).

To this purpose the embedded forms of the type "Distributed device list (\*.f45)" is available in the [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) for the representation of contact images at the components and in the [schematic paths](Glossary_o_schaltplanpfade.htm).

Form-based contact images offer the following advantages:

- The table structure can be formed user-specifically, for example by specifying the color of the table [lines](Glossary_o_leitungen.htm).
- Contact image tables can be adapted to the standard used during designing.
- Various different [contact image](Glossary_o_kontaktspiegel.htm) tables can be used within a project.
- The [cross-references](Glossary_o_querverweise.htm) displayed in the contact image behave like hyperlinks with which the respective counterpieces in the schematic can be jumped to.
- The forms can also be used for reporting purposes.
