### Routing ranges

Mechanics

Routing ranges are part of the routing path network. In routing ranges, connections are not routed along a routing path, but always it is the shortest possible and direct connection that is routed. This way, for example, it possible to implement wiring on the back of a mounting panel. No items must be located in or protrude into routing ranges. The routing range is integrated with the routing path network through routing paths terminating at its edges.
