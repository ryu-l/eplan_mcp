# New Licensing Technology

With Version 2.8 we have changed the licensing of our products to a new licensing technology.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/benefit.png)Benefit:

The new licensing technology means that you can, for example, also work without a dongle in the future.

Our new licensing technology supports the last five EPLAN versions, meaning retroactively all including Version 2.5. If you use an older version than this, please contact your EPLAN contact person who will be pleased to help you. Further information about the new licensing technology and supported EPLAN versions is available on the EPLAN website "[The new licensing technology for EPLAN](https://www.eplan.help/en-US/Infoportal/Content/misc/EPLAN_License/Content/htm/License_home.htm)".
