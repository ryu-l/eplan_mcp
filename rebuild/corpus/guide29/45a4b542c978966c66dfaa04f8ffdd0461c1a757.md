# Optimizing Nets Automatically

With automatic optimization, the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) of the net / [daisy chains](Glossary_o_verbindungsketten.htm) are redesigned in such a way that the following conditions are met:

- All [connection points](Glossary_o_anschluesse.htm) continue to be connected.
- The sum of the [wires](Glossary_o_draehte.htm) to be routed is a short as possible.
- No more than connections terminate in a connection point.
- A daisy chain is always generated.

During automatic optimization the [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) (among others the function definition) of the first connection are assigned to all the connections of the net. Fixed connections (jumpers, [direct connections](Glossary_o_direktverbindungen.htm), internal connections) remain in place and cannot be optimized.

Precondition:

You have created [routing connections](Glossary_o_verlegeverbindungen.htm). In the [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) navigator or connections navigator, a [component](Glossary_o_schaltzeichen.htm) is highlighted that contains routing connections (layout spaces, enclosures, [mounting surfaces](Glossary_o_montageflaechen.htm), 3D [part placements](Glossary_o_artikelplatzierungen.htm), connections).

1. Select the menu [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm) Project data > Connections > Optimize nets automatically.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) If the selection does not contain a net, or contains an incomplete net, an error message will be displayed. In this case, repeat and modify your selection.
2. In the Optimize nets automatically dialog, select the [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) for net optimization.
3. Click [OK].  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The [nets](Glossary_o_netze.htm) are analyzed.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The Optimize nets automatically: Preview of result dialog is displayed. The dialog shows the results of the analysis. [OK] will automatically re-[route](Glossary_o_verlegen.htm) the connections determined by the optimization.
4. In the Optimize nets automatically: Preview of result dialog, click [OK].  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The connections classified as status = "New" are routed in the new [routing tracks](Glossary_o_verlegewege.htm).
