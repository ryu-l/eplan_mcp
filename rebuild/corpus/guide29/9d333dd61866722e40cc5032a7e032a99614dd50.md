### Conduit definition lines

Fluid power

Conduit definition lines are used for the graphical representation of Fluid power bundles (e.g., conduits) in the schematic. These lines normally run orthogonally to the autoconnecting lines that you place (and which represent the conduit connections), but they can also be drawn at an angle.
