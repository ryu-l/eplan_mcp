# Enclosure Engineering with Copper Items

EPLAN Pro Panel extends the engineering of copper rails for the requirements in respect of switchgear construction.

- The shape and [form](Glossary_o_verlauf.htm) of copper [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm) can be defined individually and adjusted for the circumstances in the enclosure.
- Copper items can be bent in a flat or edgewise manner.
- Length and angle changes and associated inserting or moving of bending positions can be effected directly on the item by moving the cursor, without any detours via [settings dialogs](Glossary_o_einstellungsdialoge.htm).
- This allows for actual designing in the mounting environment.
- The manufacturing data of the individually constructed copper items can be transferred to [NC machines](Glossary_o_nc_maschinen.htm).
- Standardized copper items that are to be purchased in a fixed preset form are assigned the Prefabricated property. This makes it possible to control whether the copper items can be modified after the placement or not.
