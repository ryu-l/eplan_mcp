# P026011: 3D graphic of the part placement collides with <x>

### Cause

The 3D graphic of the [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) placement has been moved in the [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) and now collides with the 3D graphic for an enclosure, a mounting panel, a mounting rail or another part placement. In a collision, the [outlines](Glossary_o_konturen.htm) of the [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm) "merge" into one another.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/example.png)[![Closed](../../Skins/Default/Stylesheets/Images/transparent.gif)Example:](javascript:void(0);)

Collision between two adjacent [part placements](Glossary_o_artikelplatzierungen.htm) on a mounting rail.

![](../Pictures/Visualisation/ALL/messages_026011_bsp1_av.png)

<x> indicates the part designation and the displayed DT of the part placement with which the collision has occurred.

### Solution

1. From the Message management dialog, locate the part placement that caused the collision in the layout space by double clicking on the message.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The part placement in question will be highlighted in color.
2. Hold down the left mouse button and drag the part placement away, then place it on the mounting panel or mounting rail in a position where it no longer causes a collision. You can change the 3D viewpoint or rotate the viewing angle to help you prevent another collision.
3. Then start a new check run.
