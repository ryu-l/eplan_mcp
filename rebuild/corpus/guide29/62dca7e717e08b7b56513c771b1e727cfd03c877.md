# Structure boxes: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_strukturkaesten.htm)

### Structure boxes

Electrical engineering

Reflect only the graphical properties of the black box, can have a DT, but are not devices. In addition, they cannot have an part number. Everything inside a structure box is allocated to a different identifier block than that which is defined in the page properties. The components then have the same relationship to the structure box as they normally have to the page.
