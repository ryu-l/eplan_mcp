# Master / Slaves: Definition of Term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_master_slaves.htm)

### Master / slaves

Electrical engineering

A method of managing access to a common resource in a PLC configuration or a computer network. One participant is the master. This is the only participant with the right to access the common resource at any time without warning. All other participants are slaves. These cannot access the common resource whenever they wish but must wait until they receive a query from the master.
