### Phase busbars

Electrical engineering

Devices are generally connected to each other by way of single or multi-phase phase busbars to minimize the wiring effort. These phase busbars are available from the device manufacturers as accessories for devices. For connections, there is the "Phase busbar connection" function definition. A phase busbar connection is a connection that is realized via a phase busbar; it is similar to a saddle jumper.
