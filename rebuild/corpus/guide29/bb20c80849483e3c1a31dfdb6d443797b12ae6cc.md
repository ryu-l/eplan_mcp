### Guide rails

Mechanics

A user-defined rail can have several guide rails on the front. Therefore, additional logical rails are placed on the actual graphic of the outline rail that define the guide rails. Guide rails are stated as text points with the designation "Guide rail". The placement point of the text in the 3D rail is extruded into a "logical rail". Graphically, the logical rail is represented as a thin object.
