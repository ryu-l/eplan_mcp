# Conduit connections: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_paketverbindungen.htm)

### Conduit connections

Fluid power

A connection that is part of a conduit, such as a tube in a conduit. For a connection to be assigned to a conduit in EPLAN, this connection must have the "Conduit" value assigned to the Connection: Association property.
