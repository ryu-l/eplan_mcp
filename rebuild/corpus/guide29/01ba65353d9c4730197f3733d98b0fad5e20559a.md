### Symbol cross-references

Electrical engineering

Two symbols (components) of the same device are automatically cross-referenced if they contain the same device tag. The connections of the symbols, which represent the connection points of the device, must have different connection point designations. In the symbol cross-reference, the individual symbols are shown as one main element and one or more auxiliary elements.
