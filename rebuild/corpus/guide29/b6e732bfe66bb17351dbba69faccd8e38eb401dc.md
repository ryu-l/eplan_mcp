# Permanently Showing the Flow Direction of the Piping Route

The specified [pipings](Glossary_o_rohrleitungen.htm) in the [P&I diagram](Glossary_o_ri_fliessbild.htm) can already be temporarily highlighted in color by using the menu item Piping route. This also displays the flow direction. In the new version you now also have the possibility to permanently show the flow directions of the pipings and print them. Two new special [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm) are available (AR1 // 302, AR2 // 303) in the symbol library SPECIAL to this purpose.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/benefit.png)Benefit:

The piping [route](Glossary_o_verlegen.htm) of pipings in the P&I diagram can be permanently shown and printed by means of the new symbols for the flow direction.

Select one of the symbols in the Symbol selection dialog and place it on the [autoconnecting](Glossary_o_autoconnecting.htm) line of the desired piping. To ensure that the direction arrow points in the correct direction, the selected variant of the symbol may have to be adjusted by means of the Update detailed planning or Piping route functionalities to the piping route.
