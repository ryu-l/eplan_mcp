### Trades

Fluid power

The trade determines which technology should be used or selected: hydraulics, pneumatics, cooling, lubrication, cooling lubricant, gas engineering from fluid power as well as electrical engineering, mechanical engineering or process engineering. In the case of functions from fluid power the respective fluid-power trade can be selected from the list of a property. At connections, the trade is automatically determined from the targets of the functions connected.
