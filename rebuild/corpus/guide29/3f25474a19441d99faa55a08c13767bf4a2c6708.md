# Changing the Hierarchy Structure in a Layout Space Navigator per Drag & Drop

During the creation of [master data](Glossary_o_stammdaten.htm) in a macro project it is now possible to change the hierarchy structure of the [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm) in the [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) navigator per Drag & Drop. This is especially relevant for enclosures and the enclosure components belonging to the standard scope of delivery. This way, for example, an enclosure door in the item hierarchy can not be assigned to the enclosure anymore but to the "Frame profile vertical right front".

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/benefit.png)Benefit:

During the preparation of 3D [macros](Glossary_o_makros.htm) you now use Drag & Drop in the layout space navigator to individually customize the item hierarchy in enclosures.
