[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_anschlussmass.htm)

### Connection dimension

Mechanics

The connection dimension provides the measurement of the screw (e.g., M6) or of the lug (e.g., 4.8x0.5) for screw connections, threads, nipples, and plug-in connections.
