# Wiring cut-outs: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_verdrahtungsdurchbrueche.htm)

### Wiring cut-outs

Mechanics

Wiring cut-outs are manually placed rectangular openings that route connection paths to the back of a mounting surface and thus allow for wiring on the back. Routing paths are generated in wiring cut-outs automatically, that is, wiring cut-outs are part of the routing path network.
