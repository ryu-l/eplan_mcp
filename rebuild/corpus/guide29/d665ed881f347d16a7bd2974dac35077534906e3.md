# P005019: Conflict between signal and potential

### Cause

The [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) of a signal definition (which you defined for a connection potential definition point) differ from the potential definition (of the same name) defined in the schematic. The potential type, potential value or the frequency information could be different.

### Solution

Enter the corresponding properties for the connection's signal definition and the potential definition in the schematic.

Refresh the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) and then start a new check run.
