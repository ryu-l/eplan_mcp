# Network structures

The use of bus systems for measurement, regulation and controlling technology is an essential [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) of modern plant construction.

In EPLAN, the most diverse range of [network structures](Glossary_o_netzwerkstrukturen.htm) can be easily shown using [PLC boxes](Glossary_o_sps_kaesten.htm), [bus ports](Glossary_o_sps_busanschluesse.htm), [connection symbols](Glossary_o_verbindungssymbole.htm) and [cable definitions](Glossary_o_kabeldefinitionen.htm), which you insert on schematic pages in single-line or multi-line representation.

Often, standard cables for [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) are used between individual bus participants which, for reasons of simplicity, are displayed only in single-line [form](Glossary_o_verlauf.htm) and often with [bundle](Glossary_o_buendel.htm) [connection points](Glossary_o_anschluesse.htm). The detailed structure of such cables, including the required terminal [wiring](Glossary_o_verdrahtungen.htm) (e.g. [plugs](Glossary_o_stecker.htm)), is displayed by so-called [Cable assignment diagrams](cablegui_k_kabeldarstellungsplan.htm), which you can also [generate](Glossary_o_erzeugen.htm) using EPLAN. In addition to the [cable](Glossary_o_kabel.htm) graphics, these plans also contain tabular overviews in which you can store information about the usage of cables in project, part, and manufacturing data (e.g. cable type and length specifications).
