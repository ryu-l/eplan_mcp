# Connection Symbols

If the [connection points](Glossary_o_anschluesse.htm) of two [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm) are horizontally or vertically opposite one another, connection [lines](Glossary_o_leitungen.htm) are automatically drawn between them ("Autoconnecting"). Only these autoconnect lines are recognized and reported as electrical [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) between symbols. Autoconnecting is only active in schematic pages.

By inserting [connection symbols](Glossary_o_verbindungssymbole.htm) you can influence the [form](Glossary_o_verlauf.htm) of the automatic connections.

### Graphical connection symbols

Purely graphical connection symbols such as angles, T-nodes, double junctions, jumpers, and [interruption points](Glossary_o_abbruchstellen.htm) are used to display direction changes and connection line junctions in the schematic. Properties such as device, symbol, function, and parts data can't be assigned to these schematic [objects](Glossary_o_objekte.htm). The connection symbols are managed in the SPECIAL.slk symbol library. They do not have [function definitions](Glossary_o_funktionsdefinitionen.htm).

Target tracking describes the internal logic of a symbol, i.e. the connection points within a symbol between which a connection runs. It is saved as a property in the symbol.

### Logical connection symbols

In addition to purely graphical connection symbols, you can also use connection symbols that represent [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) in EPLAN. These connection symbols include [connection splicers](Glossary_o_leitungsverteiler.htm) and [line connectors](Glossary_o_leitungsverbinder.htm) or [busbars](Glossary_o_sammelschienen.htm). Properties such as device, symbol, function, and parts data can't be assigned to these. The [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) of these objects can be evaluated and e.g. output in connection lists and / or [bills of materials](Glossary_o_stuecklisten.htm).
