### Locking lists

Pre-planning

The locking list contains information about those PCT loops that have to be locked under specific conditions. "Locking" means the intentional blocking of a device by a different one. Locking is intended to prevent unintended states, sequences or events. This method is used to ensure that specific actions are not carried out unintentionally that can damage the environment, or technical devices or endanger persons.
