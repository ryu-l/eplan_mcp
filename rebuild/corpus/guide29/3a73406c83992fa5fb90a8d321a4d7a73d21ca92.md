# Aligning Elements Horizontally / Vertically in the Layout Space

The Align (horizontally) and Align (vertically) commands allow you to position several highlighted 3D [part placements](Glossary_o_artikelplatzierungen.htm) on a horizontal or vertical line. For this purpose, the 3D [objects](Glossary_o_objekte.htm) to be aligned must be on a common mounting surface or mounting rail. The line is formed from the midpoint of the two elements with the greatest distance between them. The even alignment of objects on different [mounting surfaces](Glossary_o_montageflaechen.htm) is not meaningful. Therefore, alignment is not available when objects have been highlighted that are not positioned on a common mounting surface or mounting rail.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/note.png)Note:

Note that you must run the Align on grid function after performing this action in order to reposition the insertion points on the [grid](Glossary_o_raster.htm).

Precondition:

A [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) is open. You have selected the "Front" 3D viewpoint.

1. Select the 3D objects in question, and choose the Edit > Other > Align (horizontally) or Edit > Other > Align (vertically) menu [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm).  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) EPLAN determines the centers of the two 3D objects with the greatest distance between them and aligns all elements to this line.
