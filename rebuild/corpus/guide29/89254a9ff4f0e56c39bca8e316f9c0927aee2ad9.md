# Dialog Properties

[![Closed](../../Skins/Default/Stylesheets/Images/transparent.gif)Dialog call:](javascript:void(0);)

You have opened a project.

- Project data > Parts / Devices > Device list > Popup menu > New.
- Project data > Parts / Devices > Device list. Select a [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) in the device list. Popup menu > Properties.

In this dialog you can enter a new part to be monitored as well as view and edit the [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) of an existing part.

Overview of the main dialog elements:

Part number:

Manually enter a part number here, or click [...] to open [part selection](partselectiongui_d_artikelauswahl.htm) and select a part there.

A part number may be up to 50 characters long.

Variant:

The variant is adopted from the parts management based on the part number.

ERP number:

The ERP number is adopted from parts management based on the part number.

Type number:

The type number is adopted from the parts management based on the part number.

Order number:

The order number is adopted from the parts management based on the part number.

Trade:

The trade is adopted from parts management, starting from the part number, and is prepopulated with the first placed trade setting of the part. If you need to, select another trade from the drop-down list. When [placing](Glossary_o_auflegen.htm) a device from the device list, the trade and the subtrade will be adopted in the [component](Glossary_o_schaltzeichen.htm).

Subtrade:

Here you enter the subtrade of the device if you need to. This information does not come from parts management and has to be separately entered.

Designation 1 - 3:

The [designations](Glossary_o_bezeichnungen.htm) are adopted from the parts management based on the part number.

Manufacturer:

The manufacturer is adopted from the parts management based on the part number.

Macro:

If a macro is assigned to the part, the macro name is displayed here.

Number allowed:

Enter the number of units of the current part that can be used in the project.

Total number allowed:

In this field is the sum of all Number allowed field values for all parts with the same part number.

Used unit quantity:

Indicates how often a part was actually used in the project. (The [part references](Glossary_o_artikelreferenzen.htm) are counted for this.) Deviations from the allowed quantity are permitted and will be reported in the test run.

Part section:

Here you enter the name of the part section into which the device is to be installed (for example "Conveyor element 15"). This information does not come from parts management and has to be separately entered.

Device description:

Enter a description of the functionality of the device. This information does not come from parts management and has to be separately entered. A part may occur multiply in the device list under different device descriptions.
