# EPLAN Fluid: Reports

In EPLAN Fluid [reports](formgeneratorgui_k_start.htm) of the project can be generated. For example device tag lists or parts lists can be output or also connection-specific [reports](Glossary_o_auswertungen.htm) such as piping lists or tubing lists or conduit / line plans. EPLAN Fluid offers the possibility of using numerous [report types](formgeneratorgui_k_auswertungstypen.htm).

Reports are generated using schemes. Filter, sorting, and page break criteria can be stored in these [schemes](Glossary_o_schemata.htm) for the [objects](Glossary_o_objekte.htm) to be evaluated. After the installation of EPLAN Fluid predefined schemes for the output of different reports are already available. In addition to the possibility of using predefined schemes, you can define your own schemes and adapt the reports completely individually to your requirements.
