# P026097: The part placement lies outside the optimum air-conditioned area

### Cause

The center of a [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) placement lies outside the area that can be cooled optimally by the climate control [component](Glossary_o_schaltzeichen.htm).

### Solution

1. Locate the respective part placement in the [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) using the Go to (graphic) function from the popup menu in the Message management dialog.
2. Move the center of the part placement into the optimally air-conditioned area or place the corresponding [accessories](Glossary_o_zubehoer.htm) for the air guides of the climate control component.
3. Then start a new check run.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/note.png)Note:

The climate control component itself and its accessories for the air guidance, all the mechanical parts and all the [part placements](Glossary_o_artikelplatzierungen.htm) that are placed completely outside the enclosure are excluded from this check.

The climate control component and the part placements have to be assigned to an identical air-conditioning field (Property Power dissipation: Air-conditioning field (automatic) (ID36046)).
