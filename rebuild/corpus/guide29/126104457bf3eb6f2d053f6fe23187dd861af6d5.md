### Cable / tubing protection

User interface / operation

Cable protection or tubing protection protects cables and individual connections or tubes against external influences, such as vibrations, heat, moisture, friction, fraying, bending, etc. For this purpose, spiral coiled tubes made from plastic or metal, fabric hoses, protective tubes, etc. are frequently used.
