### Spares

Electrical engineering

Spare items are elements that are not drawn in the schematic. Spares can, if requested, be included in the bill of materials output. Any type of device can be managed as spares: Terminals, cables, contactors, motors, PLC, and so on. Thus, spares are not shown either in the schematic or on overview pages or mounting panels.
