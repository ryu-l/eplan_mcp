### Drilling templates

Mechanics

Drilling templates are reports of machining. They are output in the form of a PDF file, and contain an illustration of the items and drill holes that generate the items from a drilling pattern or, manually placed, on the basic item. The drill holes are dimensioned, and contain coordinate information. When printed out, drilling templates can be used as templates for manual drilling.
