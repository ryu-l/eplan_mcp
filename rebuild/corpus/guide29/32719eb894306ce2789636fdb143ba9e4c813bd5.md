# P026095: At least two cut-outs overlap completely or partially

### Cause

Two [cut-outs](Glossary_o_durchbrueche.htm) (rectangles, [slotted holes](Glossary_o_langloecher.htm) or [drill holes](Glossary_o_bohrungen.htm), etc.) were placed on a mounting surface so that they overlap either completely or partially.

### Solution

1. Locate the two cut-outs in the [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) by using the Go to (graphic) and Go to 2nd coordinate [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) from the popup menu of the Message management dialog.
2. Move one or bothe cut-outs on the mounting surface so that they no longer overlap, or delete one of the cut-outs.
3. Then start a new check run.
