# Optimizing Nets Manually

Nets can be connected in the multi-line planning:

- By means of [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) that have the Net-connecting property enabled
- By means of their potential transmission
- or by means of jumpers.

The logic of the net transmission can be used also for [part placements](Glossary_o_artikelplatzierungen.htm) through the manual optimization of a net. New conductor chains are generated. Fixed [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) (jumpers, [direct connections](Glossary_o_direktverbindungen.htm), internal connections) remain in place and cannot be optimized.

Precondition:

You have created [routing connections](Glossary_o_verlegeverbindungen.htm). One complete net is highlighted in the [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) navigator or in the connections navigator.

1. Select the menu [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm) Project data > Connections > Optimize net manually.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) All [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) placements to be connected are colored red.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) Part placements not involved are represented as transparent.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) All sources or targets not visible are displayed.
2. Click all red part placements, one by one, that are to be connected to each other.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) All part placements that have only one connection point in the net and two connections are colored green.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) Part placements that have one connection point in the net and one connection are colored yellow.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) If a part placement has several [connection points](Glossary_o_anschluesse.htm) in the net, clicking it will display a list of connection points from which you can select a connection point.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) Connections are generated in the sequence of clicking.
3. To define a new chain, press the [N] key.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) Jumpered red part placements and those at the start and at the end that have only a connected connection are highlighted in yellow. They can be used once again to close a ring or to start a new chain.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) After clicking the last red part placement, a check is run to see whether the net is a complete single block.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The Optimize net manually dialog is displayed; here you can define whether the last chain is to be closed to [form](Glossary_o_verlauf.htm) a ring and whether the new connections are to be routed.
4. In the Optimize net manually dialog, click [OK].  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The generated connections are routed in the new [routing tracks](Glossary_o_verlegewege.htm).
