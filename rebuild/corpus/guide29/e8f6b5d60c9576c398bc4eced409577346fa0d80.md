# Printing

Project pages can be printed from the page navigator or from the graphical editor. You can use the print [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) to adapt the output of pages to different printers. The following is possible:

- You can print out any selected pages or all of the pages from the chosen project.
- Besides project pages, you can also print [symbol libraries](Glossary_o_symbolbibliotheken.htm), [macro projects](Glossary_o_makroprojekte.htm), [plot frames](Glossary_o_normblaetter.htm), and [forms](Glossary_o_formulare.htm).
- The print can be in black and white, color, or in gray scales, and can either be sent to a printer or to a file.
- You have the option of changing the output of the project pages by using different settings.
- In the print preview, you can check the effects of your settings before printing. This saves you a lot of paper and time, since no unnecessary test printouts must be made.
- Attached documents can be separately printed. These are [external documents](Glossary_o_fremddokumente.htm) inserted into schematic pages via hyperlinks or entered into the parts used on the page.
