# Automatic Detection of Hyperlinks

If a hyperlink is entered as a value of a property, this property text is now automatically recognized as a hyperlink while being placed on a page in the graphical editor. This also [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) if the text of this property is output in [reports](Glossary_o_auswertungen.htm) by means of a placeholder text. To be able to jump to the linked website, keep the [Ctrl] key pressed and click the hyperlink in the graphical editor. In an exported PDF such texts are also treated as hyperlinks.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/benefit.png)Benefit:

Thanks to the improved detection of hyperlinks you can also store the desired hyperlinks on [report pages](Glossary_o_auswertungsseiten.htm) such parts lists or device tag lists.
