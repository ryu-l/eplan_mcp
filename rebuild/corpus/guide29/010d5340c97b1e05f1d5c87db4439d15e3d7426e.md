# P002013: Too many connections at the plug-in side

### Cause

You have placed a connector with separate male and female pin representations in the schematic. A corresponding symbol with a direct connection point is used for male and [female pins](Glossary_o_steckerbuchsen.htm).

Male and female [pins](Glossary_o_steckerkontakte.htm) have furthermore the same DT, are automatically assigned to the respective male and female pin side, and have the correct function definition. At the male or female pin side, a pin did, however, receive an already assigned designation so that it exists more than once in the plug.

### Solution

Change the designation of the relevant pin so that it only exists once (per male / female pin pair) in the plug.

Refresh the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) and then start a new check run.
