# Tab Prices / Other

[![Closed](../../Skins/Default/Stylesheets/Images/transparent.gif)Dialog call:](javascript:void(0);)

Utilities > Parts > Management. You have marked a [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) in the tree or list.

In this tab, you can enter sales information which is independent of the part type and product group selected ([component](Glossary_o_schaltzeichen.htm), [cable](Glossary_o_kabel.htm), etc.). This data can be specified once per part.

Overview of the main dialog elements:

Quantity unit:

Select the appropriate unit for the part from the drop-down list, e.g., item, m, mm². You can also enter a new unit manually.

Quantity/packaging:

Enter the number of units here which are contained in one packaging unit.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/example.png)[![Closed](../../Skins/Default/Stylesheets/Images/transparent.gif)Example:](javascript:void(0);)

A shipping carton might contain 10 light bulbs; in this case, the Quantity/packaging is "10".

Purchase price/price unit / Purchase price/packaging:

There are two input fields available for two different currencies. The currency units you defined as Currency 1 and Currency 2 under Options > Settings > User > Management > Parts > [Currency] are displayed (e.g., "EUR", "USD", etc.). If the Purchase price/price unit check box is selected under [Extras] > Currencies, the price is converted automatically.

Barcode number / type:

Barcodes are used to give goods a unique identification. There are several coding standard in use internationally; EAN is the most well-known. EAN stands for "International Article Number" (previously "European Article Number"); it is a product identification for traded parts. The EAN is a number consisting of 13 or 8 digits that are centrally managed and assigned to the manufacturer upon request. In early 2009, the EAN was renamed as the GTIN (Global Trade Item Number). In addition to this, companies use company-specific or separate industry standards.

The content of these two fields can be included in part [assemblies](Glossary_o_baugruppen.htm) (parts lists, manufacturer / supplier lists, etc.) and when exporting and importing parts. The two [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) Barcode number ([ID](Glossary_o_id.htm) 22208) and Barcode type (ID 22209) are provided (within the parts data) as [placeholder texts](Glossary_o_platzhaltertexte.htm) in the relevant [forms](Glossary_o_formulare.htm) and as [format elements](Glossary_o_formatelemente.htm) in the [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) for the labeling / manufacturing data export so that you can include these data in the [reports](Glossary_o_auswertungen.htm).

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/note.png)Note:

Please note that when you enter the barcode number, there is no check to ensure it is unique. The data is output in the reports exactly as it is entered here. This means that no barcodes with combinations of [lines](Glossary_o_leitungen.htm) and gaps are generated.   
The contents of the two fields cannot be translated.

Certification:

A certification or testing number can be entered for each part. This is a text designation which is assigned based on a safety test. The following entry options are available in this group box:

- General: Select an entry from this drop-down list, or enter a new value for the certification manually. Up to 50 alphanumeric characters may be entered.
- UL certification: For this certification from Underwriters Laboratories Inc. there is a special entry field available. You can also enter up to 50 characters (letters and numbers). You can enter UL certifications, UL file numbers, or other test numbers here.
- VDE certification: For this certification from the Association for Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Information Technology, there is also a special entry field. You can also enter up to 50 characters (letters and numbers).
- ATEX identifier: For this certification in accordance with the ATEX Directive of the European Union there is also a dedicated entry field. You can also enter up to 50 characters (letters and numbers). (The designation ATEX is derived from the French abbreviation for "ATmosphère EXplosibles".)
- CE certification: For this certification from the Communauté Européene, i.e., the previous European Community, select the check box if this certification is present. The CE certification is a prerequisite for teh he first [placing](Glossary_o_auflegen.htm) on the market of products for which a CE certification in accordance with special EU directives is required, namely in all member states of the European Economic Area.
