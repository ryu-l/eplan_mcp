# Linking Segments with Pages

You have the option to link [planning objects](Glossary_o_planungsobjekte.htm) and [structure segments](Glossary_o_struktursegmente.htm) with project pages. This means that you can see at the segment on which pages the corresponding data occur, for example the function, [reports](Glossary_o_auswertungen.htm), etc. assigned to the planning object.

- Information of the segment can be displayed through a plot frame property by assigning a page to a segment. This means that the information which segment is placed on the page can, for example, be displayed automatically as a page description. If several [segments](Glossary_o_segmente.htm) are contained on the page, the information of the graphically first segment is displayed.
- If pages are assigned to a segment, these are also displayed in the graphical preview.
- During the reporting of segments the page names and descriptions of the assigned pages are output as well.
- The assignment of the pages to the copies of the segment is deleted during the copying of segments or the creation of [pre-planning macros](Glossary_o_vorplanungsmakros.htm).

Preconditions:

- You have opened a project.
- You have created structure segments and planning [objects](Glossary_o_objekte.htm).
- You have opened the pre-planning navigator and the page navigator.

1. Select a segment in the pre-planning navigator or in the graphical editor.
2. Select the Edit > Properties menu [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm) and bring the Documents / Pages tab to the foreground.
3. Click ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/all_new_as.png) (New) above the Pages table.
4. Navigate through the displayed project pages and select the desired pages in the dialog Page selection.
5. Click [OK].  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The pages are entered in the list of assigned pages at the segment.
6. Select a line in the table and click ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/all_delete_as.png) (Delete) to remove the assignment of the corresponding page to the segment.
7. Click [OK].

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/info.png)Tip:

You can open a marked page in the graphical editor by using the popup menu items Open and Open in new window.
