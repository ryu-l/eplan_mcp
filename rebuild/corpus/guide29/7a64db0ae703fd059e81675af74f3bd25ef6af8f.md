# Activating Quick Snap

The Quick snap feature will help you select [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm) and [macros](Glossary_o_makros.htm) quicker, and place them more reliably on the desired [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm).

Precondition:

You have opened a page, [form](Glossary_o_verlauf.htm), plot frame or symbol.

1. Select the menu item Options > Quick snap to activate or deactivate this option.

### Select symbols by using Quick snap

1. Select the menu [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm) Options > Quick snap, to activate the Quick snap option.
2. Hold down [Q], before you move the cursor over a symbol.   
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The insertion point of the symbol will be used as the snap point, meaning, the cursor will be strongly pulled to the insertion point. This is indicated by a small circle in the graphical editor.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Visualisation/ALL/gededitgui_pointsnap_as.png)

### Place symbols by using Quick snap

1. Select the menu items Options > Quick snap, to activate the Quick snap option.
2. First select the desired action (for example Insert > Symbol), and press the [Q] key.   
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) In this case, the activated Quick snap affects the [connecting points](Glossary_o_anschlusspunkte.htm) of the symbols. The connecting points of a symbol then have the tendency to be pulled to an existing or possible connection line. This is indicated by a small double-cross in the graphical editor.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Visualisation/ALL/gededitgui_connectionsnap_as.png)  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The Quick snap is deactivated again after the action has been performed.
