### Star cross-references

Electrical engineering

Variant of the interruption point cross-reference. With a star cross-reference, one interruption point is defined as the starting point. All other interruption points of the same name refer to this starting point. At the starting point, a formattable list of the cross-references to the other interruption points is displayed. Here you can define how many cross-references are to be displayed alongside or between each other.
