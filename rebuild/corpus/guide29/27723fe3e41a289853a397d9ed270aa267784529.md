# P501020: The accessory part <x>, variant <y>, <z>, entered at the part does not exist

### Cause

In the parts database to be checked a [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) at a [main part](Glossary_o_hauptartikel.htm) was found that is entered in an accessory list, but does not exist as a record in the parts database. EPLAN specifies the name of the part in the placeholder <x>, the part variant in the placeholder <y> and the name of the accessory list in the placeholder <z>.

### Solution

1. Open the parts management and first [create](Glossary_o_erstellen.htm) the required part. Or import the part.
2. Identify the part of the Accessories tab as an accessory part by activating the Accessories check box and click [OK].
3. Mark the desired accessory list in the tree view of the parts management.
4. Bring the Part tab to the front, and select the accessory part for the accessory list in the Part number field.
5. Close the parts management, save the modified data, and carry out a parts synchronization.
6. In the message management dialog mark the line with the message and select the Properties popup menu item.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The parts management is opened, the respective main part is already marked in it.
7. Bring the Accessories tab to the front, and select the accessory list in the Part number / Name field.
8. Close the parts management, save the modified data, and carry out a parts synchronization.
9. Then start a new check run.
