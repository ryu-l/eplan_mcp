# P002008: Number of female pins and male pins in the plug do not match

### Cause

You created a plug connection with separate male and [female pins](Glossary_o_steckerbuchsen.htm) in the Plug Navigator or placed it in the schematic. The plug has two [plug definitions](Glossary_o_steckerdefinitionen.htm) with the same DT, but different [function definitions](Glossary_o_funktionsdefinitionen.htm) ("Plug definition for [male pins](Glossary_o_steckerstifte.htm)" / "Plug definition for female [pins](Glossary_o_steckerkontakte.htm)"). On the male pin side of the plug there are more pins than on the female side or vice versa.

### Solution

Add the corresponding pins to the male or female side of the plug, so that there is a corresponding number of male and female pins on each side.

Refresh the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) and then start a new check run.
