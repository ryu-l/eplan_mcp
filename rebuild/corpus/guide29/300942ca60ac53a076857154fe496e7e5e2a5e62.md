# DT Syntax Check

A device typically contains all of the identifying information (such as [higher-level function](Glossary_o_anlage.htm), [mounting location](Glossary_o_einbauort.htm), and identifier) in its device tag. An identifier check function is available for checking the length and validity of the characters in the specifications for the DT.

A check also takes place when entering the identifier in Structure identifier management. You specify which characters are permitted in the [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) for the DT syntax check. By default, the ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/all_umlauts_as.png) characters are not permitted unless they are defined as permitted special text characters in the settings.

The identifier check offers you the following options:

- You can deactivate the check, if necessary.
- You can define various settings and permissible special characters for the check of [identifier blocks](Glossary_o_kennzeichnungsbloecke.htm), device tags, and counter numbers.
- EPLAN offers you the option of ignoring an invalid character during the syntax check.
