# P027012: Several routing tracks are filled (topology, Pro Panel) at the connection from <x> to <y>

### Cause

There are separate routing track [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) on the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) for the [modules](Glossary_o_module.htm) EPLAN FieldSys and EPLAN Pro Panel. Possibly, you plan with both modules, and one and the same connection or one and the same [cable](Glossary_o_kabel.htm) was routed in the [topology](Glossary_o_topologie.htm) and [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm). In the process, the length and connection point values (e.g., in connection with [wire termination processing](Glossary_o_verbindungsende_behandlung.htm), etc.) of the last [route](Glossary_o_verlegen.htm) were overwritten.

### Solution

1. Remove the targets from one of the [representation types](Glossary_o_darstellungsarten.htm) (topology, 3D [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) placement)
2. Also remove the corresponding routing track on the connection / cable.
3. Then start a new check run.
