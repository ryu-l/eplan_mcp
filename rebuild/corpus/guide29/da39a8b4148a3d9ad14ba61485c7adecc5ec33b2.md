# Creating an Accessory List and Assigning a Main Part

Parts management provides "accessory lists" as a simple means of managing alternative accessory parts. They allow you to select a [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) from a list of possible alternatives. To this purpose , the accessory lists [form](Glossary_o_verlauf.htm) an own hierarchical level in the parts management tree view and are displayed there under their name.

Once you have created accessory lists, they may be assigned to a [main part](Glossary_o_hauptartikel.htm) of a part type in the Parts management module.

Precondition:

You have opened the parts management: Utilities > Parts > Management.

### [ClosedCreating an accessory list](javascript:void(0);)

1. Select the Accessory list hierarchy level in the tree.
2. Select the New popup menu item.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) EPLAN automatically generates a new accessory list with the name New\_<sequential\_number>.
3. On the right-hand section of the dialog change to the Accessory list tab.
4. Change the automatically assigned entry in the Name field.   
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) EPLAN automatically fills the [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) Creation date and Modification date (Properties tab) with the corresponding data. These cannot be changed.
5. Bring the Parts tab to the front.
6. Click ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/all_new_as.png) (New) to [generate](Glossary_o_erzeugen.htm) a line in the accessory list.
7. Click [...] in the Part number field.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The Part selection dialog opens. Only previously defined accessory parts are displayed through the "Accessories" default setting of the field-based filter in the tree view.
8. Select the desired accessory parts in the tree and click [OK].
9. Repeat the selection if you want to add a further accessory part.
10. Close the parts management and save the modified data.
11. Synchronize the parts data.

### [ClosedAssigning an accessory list to a main part](javascript:void(0);)

1. Mark the desired main part in the tree or in the list.
2. Bring the Accessories tab to the front.
3. Click ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/all_new_as.png) (New) to generate a line in the list of the accessory parts.
4. Click [...] in the Part number / Name field.
5. In the tree structure mark the newly created accessory list in the Accessory list hierarchy level.
6. Click [OK].
7. Close the parts management and save the modified data.
8. Synchronize the parts data.
