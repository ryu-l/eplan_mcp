# P002002: Pin without targets

### Cause

#### Possibility 1

You placed a pin in the schematic without connecting it to a [component](Glossary_o_schaltzeichen.htm).

#### Possibility 2

In the [plugs](Glossary_o_stecker.htm) navigator you have pre-defined a pin or a plug with several [pins](Glossary_o_steckerkontakte.htm) by means of the New or New [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) popup menu item.

### Solution

#### Regarding option 1

Connect at least one of the symbol [connection points](Glossary_o_anschluesse.htm) of the placed pin to a component. Refresh the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) and then start a new check run.

#### Regarding option 2

Place the pin or the pins of the plug in the schematic and proceed for each pin as described in section "Regarding option 1".
