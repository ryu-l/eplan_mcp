### Schematic paths

Electrical engineering

Plot frames are divided into multiple paths in order to find the placed components more quickly. The orientation of schematic paths (horizontal or vertical) depends on the standard. (In the geometric division of plot frames we speak of rows and columns, since this is standard-independent. Whether the column of a plot frame is seen as a path or a schematic position depends on the standard selected for the project.)
