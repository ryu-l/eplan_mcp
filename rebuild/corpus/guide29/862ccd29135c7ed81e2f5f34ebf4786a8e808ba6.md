# Exchanging Devices

When two [devices](Glossary_o_betriebsmittel.htm) are changed, all the [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) of the respective devices are exchanged. The DT is exchanged.

Preconditions:

- You have opened a project.
- You have opened a project page in the graphical editor, or you have opened a [project data](Glossary_o_projektdaten.htm) navigator.

1. Select any [component](Glossary_o_schaltzeichen.htm) / function of the two devices you wish to exchange.
2. Select the Project data > Devices > Exchange menu [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm).  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) All the functions of the two selected devices have the DT of the respective other device assigned to them..
