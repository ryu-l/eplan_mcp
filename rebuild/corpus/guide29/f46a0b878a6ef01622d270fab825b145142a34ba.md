### Normal vector

Mechanics

The normal vector of a surface is a mathematical magnitude in the calculation of three-dimensional surfaces that are aligned perpendicularly upward or downward to the base. It determines whether the surface faces the viewer or is turned away from him / her. In EPLAN Pro Panel the normal vector is used to determine whether a surface is visible or is covered by other surfaces and whether they are transparent or not.
