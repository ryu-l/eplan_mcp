[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_allgemeinebetriebsmittel.htm)

### General devices

Electrical engineering

General devices are all devices that do not have separate tabs in the property dialog, e.g., fuses, motors, contactors, etc. The other (special) devices include, for example, terminals, plugs and cables; there are special editing and reporting options for them.
