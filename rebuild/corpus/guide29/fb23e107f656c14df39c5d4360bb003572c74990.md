# Displaying Insertion Points

To edit the [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) of [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm) or texts, it is necessary to select their insertion points. The View > Insertion points option makes it easier to find the insertion points: If this option is activated, the insertion points of symbols and text are highlighted by a black square.

Precondition:

You have opened a page, [form](Glossary_o_verlauf.htm), plot frame or symbol.

1. Select the menu item View > Insertion points, or press the [i] key.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The insertion points are displayed as small squares.
2. Select the menu item View > Insertion points, or press the [i] key once more.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The insertion points are hidden again.
