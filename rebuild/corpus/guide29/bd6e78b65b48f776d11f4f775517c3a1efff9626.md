### PLC card overviews

Electrical engineering

You can display the descriptions of the connection points on PLC card overview pages. In the case of distributed display of PLC cards, the overview pages make it easier to locate the inputs / outputs within the schematic. All cross-references to the PLC connection points in the schematic are displayed in the overview.
