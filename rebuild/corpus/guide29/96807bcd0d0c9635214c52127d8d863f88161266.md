### Pair cross-references

Electrical engineering

This cross-reference refers from a component, the so-called "pair cross-reference contact", to its "wired" opposite in the schematic. In the opposite direction, the opposite partner refers back to the pair cross-reference contact (see [Pair cross-reference contacts](Glossary_o_paarquerverweiskontakte.htm)). A reference to the main function does not exist in this case.
