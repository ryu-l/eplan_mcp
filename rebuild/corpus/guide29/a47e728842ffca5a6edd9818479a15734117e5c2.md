### Hose line identification

Fluid power

The hose line identification in EPLAN fulfils the specifications of the hose line standard DIN 20066 and is made up of the values of the following data:

- Abbreviation of the manufacturer
- Value of the working pressure + Unit of the pressure indication
- Year of manufacturing + Month of manufacturing

  
Example: Hansa-Flex / 110 bar / 10/01  
The hose line identification can be evaluated and reported and can be output together with the type code in a connection list.
