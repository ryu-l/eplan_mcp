### Male pins

Electrical engineering

The male pin is the male part of the plug contact. Male pins are not protected against manual contact. Thus, no current-carrying connections may be assigned to them. This regulation is valid for all standards and for all voltages for which any risk can result from contact. (According to DIN, this is any voltage over 24V.)
