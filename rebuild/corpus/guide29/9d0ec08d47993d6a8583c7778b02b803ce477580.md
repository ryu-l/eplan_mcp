# P026027: Cut-out is not completely on a mounting surface

### Cause

A cut-out was not placed completely on the mounting surface. "Not completely" means that the cut-out with its handles lies either only partially or completely outside the mounting surface.

### Solution

1. Locate the cut-out in the [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) using the function Go to (graphic) from the popup menu in the Message management dialog.
2. Move the cut-out onto the desired mounting surface so that its handles lie completely within the [mounting surfaces](Glossary_o_montageflaechen.htm).
3. Then start a new check run.
