# P005028: Several potential definitions on one potential

### Cause

You have placed several potential definitions at a drawn connection. Both [potential definition points](Glossary_o_potenzialdefinitionspunkte.htm) and [potential connection points](Glossary_o_potenzialanschluesse.htm) are regarded as potential definitions.

### Solution

Reduce the number of potential definition points and/or [connection points](Glossary_o_anschluesse.htm) at the connection, leaving only a single valid potential definition.

Refresh the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) and then start a new check run.
