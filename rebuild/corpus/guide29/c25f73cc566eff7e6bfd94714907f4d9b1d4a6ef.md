### Workspaces

User interface / operation

An EPLAN workspace encompasses the size and position of the EPLAN main window, as well as the position, size and settings of dockable dialogs, menus and toolbars. User-defined toolbars are also saved with a workspace. Workspaces can be created at any time and then again re-selected at a later time.
