### Operational sequence sheets

User interface / operation

Operational sequence sheets are simple graphic representations that provide a structured overview of the process of a plant, a machine or a device. In EPLAN, you can create both simple text-based or symbol-based functional diagrams (see [Functional diagrams](Glossary_o_funktionsdiagramme.htm)) and also GRAFCET diagrams.
