# Schemes

A scheme is a group of [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) that are stored together under a particular name. For most settings, there are standard [schemes](Glossary_o_schemata.htm) from EPLAN that you can't edit. These are stored during the installation in their own directory (by default under \Schemes\<Company name> on the drive and in the directory where EPLAN is installed). There are for example schemes for the manufacturing data export, numbering settings for [devices](Glossary_o_betriebsmittel.htm), cables etc., [workspaces](Glossary_o_arbeitsbereiche.htm) etc.

If you nevertheless require own settings ever, you can [create](Glossary_o_erstellen.htm) your own [user-defined](Glossary_o_benutzerdefiniert.htm) schemes: Through the [toolbar](schemeconfig_m_schemaschaltflaechen.htm) next to the Scheme field, you can create schemes, store changes to a scheme, copy or delete a scheme. Schemes can also be imported or exported. A multiple selection of schemes is possible during importing, not during exporting.
