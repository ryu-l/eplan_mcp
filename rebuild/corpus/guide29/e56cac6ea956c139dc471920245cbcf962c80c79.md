# Fitting Length-variable Items

Fitting is about reconciling the lengths of length-variable [items](Glossary_o_bauteile.htm) placed in parallel to each other ([wire ducts](Glossary_o_verdrahtungskanaele.htm), [mounting rails](Glossary_o_tragschienen.htm), [busbars](Glossary_o_sammelschienen.htm), C horizontal rails, [user-defined](Glossary_o_benutzerdefiniert.htm) rails) with selectable points and edges. Possible uses are:

- All rails and wire ducts of varying lengths placed on a mounting panel are drawn towards an edge of the mounting panel in a suitable manner in a single work step.
- All rails and wire ducts of varying lengths placed next to a vertical wire duct are drawn towards it in a suitable manner in a single work step.
- All rails and wire ducts of varying lengths placed between two vertical wire ducts are drawn towards them in a suitable manner in two work steps.

Preconditions:

- You have opened a project.
- The [layout space](Glossary_o_bauraum.htm) navigator is open and a layout space is open.
- The layout space contains several length-variable items.

1. Select the Edit > Graphic > Fit rails / wire ducts menu items.
2. Select the items to be fitted by surrounding them with a rectangle.
3. Move the cursor over the edge of a vertical wire duct or over the side section of the mounting panel.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) When the cursor touches a suitable edge, it is highlighted. If the cursor touches a suitable point, it is displayed as a highlighted square.
4. Click to select the desired point or edge.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The selected items are lengthened or shortened jointly up to the selected edge / selected point.
