# Form Functions: Alignment Boxes for Placeholders

Placeholders can be inserted into [forms](Glossary_o_formulare.htm), which are replaced by text or graphics (e.g. jumpers) in the report tables.

Especially for terminal [connection diagrams](Glossary_o_anschlussplaene.htm), different information can be graphically output:

- Any desired, but fixed graphic, e.g. for "screw [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm)"
- Variable graphics per record, e.g. for special terminal types (PE / PEN)
- Generated graphics for [cable lines](Glossary_o_kabellinien.htm), [device boxes](Glossary_o_bm_kaesten.htm), and [connection forks](Glossary_o_verbindungsgabeln.htm)
- Symbol graphics.

It is sometimes necessary for the graphic to be scaled and for size limits to be pre-defined in the [form](Glossary_o_verlauf.htm). The graphic can also be oriented when it is placed in your alignment box. These [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) are made under the Alignment box hierarchical level on the Format tab.

Two further settings are possible to align text, in addition to this orientation of the alignment box. These are also made on the Format tab, but in this case under the Text box hierarchical level.
