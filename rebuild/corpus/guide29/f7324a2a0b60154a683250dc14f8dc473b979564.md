### Address assignment

Electrical engineering

Overview of the address ranges of a PLC card, can be displayed on report pages of the type "PLC card overview". PLC cards can have separated address ranges for inputs and outputs. The address range is specified in EPLAN on the PLC card by specifying a start address and the associated data length.
