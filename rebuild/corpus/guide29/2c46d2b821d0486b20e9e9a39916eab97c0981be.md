# Cooling lubricant: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_kuehlschmiermittel.htm)

### Cooling lubricant

Fluid power

Cooling lubricants are used during the processing of materials. They primarily serve to reduce friction through lubrication, to extract heat through cooling and to remove filings through flushing the working area. Water, oils or emulsions are used as cooling lubricants.
