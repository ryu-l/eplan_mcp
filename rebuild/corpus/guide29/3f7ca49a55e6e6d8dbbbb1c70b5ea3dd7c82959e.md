# Complex Devices in Reports

In particular in the energy branch there are complex [devices](Glossary_o_betriebsmittel.htm) that - depending on the height of the voltage - can be composed from a large number of [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) and devices.

In EPLAN such complex devices are managed as [macros](Glossary_o_makros.htm) in which the internal circuiting between the inputs and outputs is depicted. In the process property placements are stored at the [connection points](Glossary_o_anschluesse.htm) (Placement property) in the macro. These are converted during the "Device tag list" report, where the macro is placed on a page, into [placeholder texts](Glossary_o_platzhaltertexte.htm). This makes simple jumping to the corresponding page / connection point possible.
