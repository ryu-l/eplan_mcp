# Mounting surfaces NC: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_montageflaechen_nc.htm)

### Mounting surfaces NC

Mechanics

Area on an outline that can be used for NC machining. The field size of the mounting surface NC signals to the NC machine the size of the item to be machined and the location of the surface to be machined on the machine.
