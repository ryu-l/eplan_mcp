# Specifying Settings for Displaying the Connection Symbols

The general [project-specific settings](Glossary_o_projektbezogeneeinstellungen.htm) for graphical editing include various areas that influence the work in the graphical editor.

Precondition:

You have opened a project.

1. Options > Settings > Projects > "Project name" > Graphical editing > General
2. In the Display connection junctions as group box specify how [connection symbols](Glossary_o_verbindungssymbole.htm) are to be displayed in the graphical editor.
3. Specify other general [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) by selecting / deselecting the check boxes.
4. Click [OK].  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The connection [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm) are displayed accordingly.
