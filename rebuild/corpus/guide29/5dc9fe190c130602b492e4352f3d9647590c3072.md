# Potentials and Signals

In EPLAN there is a difference drawn between [potentials](Glossary_o_potenziale.htm) and [signals](Glossary_o_signale.htm). Through the potentials, both the templates for the connection [properties](Glossary_o_eigenschaften.htm) and the potential information are introduced to the project.

- Potentials and signals can be separately defined.
- Potentials and signals can be defined by [potential definition points](Glossary_o_potenzialdefinitionspunkte.htm) or [potential connection points](Glossary_o_potenzialanschluesse.htm).
- Potentials and signals allow all [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) with the same potential to be assigned the same properties.
- Connection definition points allow the properties of individual connections, defined by the potential properties, to be overwritten.
- Potentials, signals, and [nets](Glossary_o_netze.htm) can be temporarily highlighted in color to provide a quick overview of their extent.
- Connections with different potential types can be given colors in the [settings](Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm) of the [layers](Glossary_o_ebenen.htm) assigned to them.
