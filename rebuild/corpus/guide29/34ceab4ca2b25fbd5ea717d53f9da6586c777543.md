### Segment templates

Pre-planning

Segment templates serve as a template for the creation of new structure segments, planning objects, like function templates serve as a template for functions. You can predefine all the relevant data for a segment in a segment template and store them project-specifically. During assignment of a segment template its properties are transferred to the segment.
