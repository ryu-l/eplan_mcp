# Descriptive mounting location: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_beschreibendereinbauort.htm)

### Descriptive mounting location

Electrical engineering

If the mounting location (see Mounting location) is descriptive (i.e., it is displayed in the DT), but not identifying for the project structure, then we speak of a "Descriptive mounting location".
