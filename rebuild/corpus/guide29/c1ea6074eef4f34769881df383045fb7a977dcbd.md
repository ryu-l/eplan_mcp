# Toolbars

As standard, the [toolbars](Glossary_o_symbolleisten.htm) are located below the menu bar and consist of buttons allowing you to directly call up the most important EPLAN [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm). Toolbars can be revealed and hidden using the popup menu: Click an empty area on the menu or toolbar with the right mouse button and all available toolbars will then be displayed and can be selected / deselected.

Non-available toolbar [symbols](Glossary_o_symbole.htm) are displayed grayed out.

As "dockable" control elements, you can position the toolbars anywhere on the screen, even outside the EPLAN [main window](Glossary_o_hauptfenster.htm), by clicking and holding the left mouse button on the toolbar title bar and then pulling it to the desired position.

### Customize toolbars

When you move the cursor over a symbol in a toolbar, then a small text window is displayed below the symbol with a description. Whether or not these so-called Tooltip boxes are displayed depends on the appropriate setting in the Customize dialog.

The Customize dialog also allows you to adjust and extend the toolbars. It is also possible to combine frequent commands into a single toolbar. In order to retain your personal and / or adjusted toolbars when changing [workspaces](Glossary_o_arbeitsbereiche.htm), you must save these as [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) of the current workspace.
