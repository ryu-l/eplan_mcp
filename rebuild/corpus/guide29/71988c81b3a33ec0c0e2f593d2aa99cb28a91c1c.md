### DIN identifiers

Electrical engineering

A complete DIN identifier consists of the identifier blocks "Functional assignment" (prefix "=="), "Higher-level function" (prefix "="), "Installation site" (prefix "++"), "Mounting location" (prefix "+"), and "Device" (prefix "-").
