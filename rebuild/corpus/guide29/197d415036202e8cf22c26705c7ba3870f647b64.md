### Single-line representations

Electrical engineering

A simplified representation of schematics, which uses a reduced number of connection points (one or a maximum of two) for the components. Parallel connections or cable connections are reduced to a single line. Bundled representation of connections and cables is also normal. The single-line representation is often used for plant overviews.
