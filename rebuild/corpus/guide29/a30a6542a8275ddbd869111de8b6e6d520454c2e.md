# Function-related Reports

In contrast to [Report overviews](formgeneratorgui_k_auswertungsuebersicht.htm), function-related [reports](Glossary_o_auswertungen.htm) have a device in the header as standard, e.g., a terminal strip. The associated functions are in the data area, e.g., the terminals of a terminal strip.

Function-related reports can only occur once in the project.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/example.png)[![Closed](../../Skins/Default/Stylesheets/Images/transparent.gif)Example:](javascript:void(0);)

A terminal diagram of terminal strip X1 may occur only once in the project.
