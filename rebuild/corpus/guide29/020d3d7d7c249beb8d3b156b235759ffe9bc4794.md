# Offline Numbering

EPLAN allows you to renumber multiple [devices](Glossary_o_betriebsmittel.htm). You determine the range of devices to be numbered and select a numbering format. Based on these specifications the devices are numbered offline by EPLAN.

- You can number all devices in the entire project or selected devices on selected pages.
- You can number placed or unplaced devices.
- You can use predefined [numbering formats](Glossary_o_nummerierungsformate.htm) or define your own numbering [formats](Glossary_o_formate.htm).
- After you have numbered the devices you can manually adjust the numbering of individual devices.
- If logical problems occur during the device numbering, a message is generated.
