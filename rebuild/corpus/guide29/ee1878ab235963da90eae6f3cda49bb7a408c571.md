# Head stations: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_kopfstationen.htm)

### Head stations

Electrical engineering

A head station is the first PLC card of a station (also "field bus station") and includes the bus ports. Head stations are sometimes also called coupler modules.

When configuring in EPLAN a head station is also the rack for the PLC cards (also "periphery modules") that are attached at its side. Head stations are always used within bus systems.
