### PLC connection points

Electrical engineering

Reflects a device connection point but has some extra information (e.g., the symbolic address). A PLC connection point is drawn in the schematic in the same manner as a "normal" terminal. It is, however, only characterized by the connection point properties, since it is not a separate item but rather a connection point to a PLC card. The connection point designation is always allocated to a PLC card.
