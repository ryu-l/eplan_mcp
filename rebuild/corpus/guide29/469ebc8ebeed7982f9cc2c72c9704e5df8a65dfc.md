# Online Numbering

EPLAN can automatically number [devices](Glossary_o_betriebsmittel.htm) when they are inserted into the schematic. The appropriate device tag is determined online and is immediately available on insertion. Online numbering offers you the following possibilities:

- New and copied devices can be online numbered when they are inserted into the schematic. The devices automatically receive a DT and the counter is incremented.
- Macro devices can be online numbered when they are inserted into the schematic.
- You can use predefined [numbering formats](Glossary_o_nummerierungsformate.htm) or define your own numbering [formats](Glossary_o_formate.htm).
- The [online numbering mode](Glossary_o_online_nummerierungsmodus.htm) allows you to disable [online numbering](Glossary_o_onlinenummerieren.htm) for all devices.
- The online numbering formats allow you to disable online numbering for selected device groups.
