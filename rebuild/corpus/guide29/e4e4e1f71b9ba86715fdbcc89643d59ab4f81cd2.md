# Device navigator

The device navigator gives you a logical overview of your [project data](Glossary_o_projektdaten.htm):

- You can filter project data in order to more easily locate it.
- You can configure different views for project data.
- You can recognize whether [functions](Glossary_o_funktionen.htm) have been placed or not.
- You can insert, edit, delete, or place functions.
