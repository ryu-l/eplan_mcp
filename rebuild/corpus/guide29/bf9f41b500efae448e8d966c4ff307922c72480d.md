### Device connection points

Electrical engineering

A device connection point is drawn in the schematic in the same manner as a "normal" terminal. It has a DT and a connection point designation, a connection point description, and all other properties that all (normal) functions have. It is, however, only characterized by the connection point properties, since it is not a separate item but rather a connection point to a device.
