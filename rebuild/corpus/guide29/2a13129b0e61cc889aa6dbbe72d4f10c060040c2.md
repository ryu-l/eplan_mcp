### 3D viewpoints

Mechanics

3D viewpoints are different fixed views onto the 3D view of the layout space. There are six different orthogonal (top, bottom, left, right, front, back) and 4 isometric (south-west, south-east, north-east, north-west) views. The designations of the four isometric viewpoints determine the orientation from which the model is viewed. Additionally, the viewpoint is rotated 60° downwards. Using the Rotate viewing angle function, you can set any intermediate stages between the fixed 3D viewpoints.
