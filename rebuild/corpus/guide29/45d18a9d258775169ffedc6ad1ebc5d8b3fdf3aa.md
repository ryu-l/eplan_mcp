# P028014: The planning object has parts with templates and at least one additional template of its own

### Cause

At least one template was defined at a planning object, and additionally at least one [part](Glossary_o_artikel.htm) with templates has been selected.

### Solution

1. Locate the planning object in the pre-planning navigator using the Synchronize selection function offered by the popup menu in the Message management dialog.
2. Delete the excess templates, or delete the parts.
3. Then start a new check run.
