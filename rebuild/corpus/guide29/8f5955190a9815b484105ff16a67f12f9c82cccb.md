[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_sicherheitsfunktionen.htm)

### Safety functions

User interface / operation

The safety function is a term taken from safety engineering. It involves the correct functioning of control and protection devices of a machine or higher-level function. In EPLAN safety functions are functions that, for example, take up or trigger a safety-relevant signal. This can, for example, be a PLC safety input or also a contact of a safety device.
