### Sub-identifiers

Electrical engineering

In addition to the DIN standard specifications, particular EPLAN identifier blocks can have one or more sub-identifiers. A sub-identifier is always assigned to a main identifier (see [Main identifiers](Glossary_o_hauptkennzeichen.htm)). The sub-identifiers form a hierarchy with the corresponding main identifier.
