# Settings: Definition of term

[Open topic with navigation](../../EPLAN_Help.htm#htm/Glossary_o_einstellungen.htm)

### Settings

User interface / operation

Settings affect the behavior of object properties as well as the program behavior; they are identified by name (see [Schemes](Glossary_o_schemata.htm)), and cannot be output in forms, etc. You can customize the program to suit your personal requirements and area of work by means of the settings.
