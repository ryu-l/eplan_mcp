# Multi-user Monitor

The multi-user monitor helps you to see which other uses are active on which project. You will see at a glance the [actions](Glossary_o_aktionen.htm) of other users in the project, thus allowing you to efficiently coordinate the project work.

If delays occur during project editing, you can also use the multi-user monitor to find out whether the problems are caused by the [multi-user operation](Glossary_o_multiuser_betrieb.htm).
