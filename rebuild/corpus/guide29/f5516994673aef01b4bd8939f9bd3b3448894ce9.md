# P015004: The form '<x>' is used in the form '<y>', but is not contained in the project

### Cause

For your [reports](Glossary_o_auswertungen.htm) you use a [form](Glossary_o_verlauf.htm) <y> for which a next form <x> is specified. However, the next form is not stored in the current project.

### Solution

Store the next form <x> in your project.
