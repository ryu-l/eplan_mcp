# P001016: Terminal without targets

### Cause

You placed a terminal in the schematic without connecting it to a [component](Glossary_o_schaltzeichen.htm).

### Solution

Connect at least one of the symbol [connection points](Glossary_o_anschluesse.htm) of the placed terminal to a component.

Refresh the [connections](Glossary_o_verbindungen.htm) and then start a new check run.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/note.png)Note:

Note that unplaced terminals are not captured by this check run!
