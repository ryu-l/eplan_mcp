# P005028: Several potential definitions on one potential

### Cause

You have inserted multiple potential definitions within a potential. Potential definition points and potential connection points are considered as potential definitions.

### Solution

1. Locate the potential definitions reported as faulty in the schematic by using the functionalities Go to (graphic) and Go to (2nd coordinate), which the popup menu of the Message management dialog offers you.
2. With the exception of one valid potential definition, delete all potential definition points and / or potential connection points. Or enter the same potential name at all potential definitions.
3. If required, start a new check run.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/info.png)Tip:

To highlight the form of potentials in the schematic in color, which also makes the tracking of potentials easier, you can use the [Potential tracking](potentialbrowsergui_h_potenzialverfolgung.htm) in Eplan. To this purpose select the button Potential tracking in the command group General in the View tab.
