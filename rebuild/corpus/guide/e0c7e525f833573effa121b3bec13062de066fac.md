# Highlighting the Potentials, Signals, or Nets

Potentials, signals, and nets can be temporarily highlighted in color to provide a quick overview of their extent. The currently-selected potential, signal, or net is then colored according to the colors you defined in the user settings.

The highlighting of potentials is described in the following section; signals and networks can be highlighted in the same way by means the following commands: Tab View > Command group General > Signal tracking or Net tracking.

Precondition:

You have defined the color to be used to highlight the potential / signal / net in the user settings (File > Settings > User > Graphical editing > 2D in the drop-down list Track potential).

1. Select the button Potential tracking in the command group General in the View tab.
2. Click a connection symbol or a connection line.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) Eplan searches for the associated potential, and colors this completely (i.e., even across page boundaries).
3. Click another connection.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The highlighting of the first potential is removed, and the potential associated with this connection is colored.
4. Deactivate the Potential tracking button.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The highlighting of the potential is removed.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/note.png)Notes:

- Color highlights for potentials, signals, or nets can only be removed when the connections are active. Please note that the potential data is only written to the connections during a project-wide update of the connections. In the case of a page change, only the connection data is updated.
- Once you have ended the potential, signal, or net tracking by using [Esc] or via the popup menu Cancel action, you must switch the tracking off and on again once (for example by using the following commands: Tab View > Command group General > Potential tracking) before you can highlight a potential, signal, or network in color once more.
