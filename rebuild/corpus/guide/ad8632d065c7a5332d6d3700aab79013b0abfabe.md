# Tab Mounting rails

[![Closed](../../Skins/Default/Stylesheets/Images/transparent.gif)Dialog call:](javascript:void(0);)

Tab Master data > Command group Part > Management. You have selected a part in the tree view or list view from the "Mechanics" Generic product group, "Busbars" Product group, "Adapters" Product subgroup. Select the Mounting rails tab.

In this tab you define the specific data for adapter mounting rails, meaning for parts from the "Mechanics" Generic product group, "Busbars" Product group, "Adapters" Product subgroup. The data can be specified more than once per part, namely once per variant.
