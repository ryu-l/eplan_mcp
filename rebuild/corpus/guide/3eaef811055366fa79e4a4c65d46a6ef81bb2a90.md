# Eplan Fluid: DT Numbering

Eplan Fluid offers a number of methods for numbering fluid device tags:

- [Online Numbering](numerationgui_k_start.htm)
- [Offline numbering](offlinenumerationgui_k_start.htm)
- Numbering by means of the dialog [DT selection](modaldialogsdb_d_bmkauswahl.htm).

If you use a device tag in accordance with ISO 1219- 2 for fluid devices, the item number is incremented by default during the insertion of symbols. When inserting macros or copies you have the possibility to specify how the program is to proceed during the numbering of the fluid DTs. You can increment either the circuit number or the item number.

During the DT selection of fluid devices identified in accordance with ISO 1219-2 it is possible to select the next free circuit number or item number with program support in the visible device tag.
