# P026097: The part placement lies outside the optimum air-conditioned area

### Cause

The center of a part placement lies outside the area that can be cooled optimally by the climate control component.

### Solution

1. Locate the respective part placement in the layout space using the Go to (graphic) function from the popup menu in the Message management dialog.
2. Move the center of the part placement into the optimally air-conditioned area or place the corresponding accessories for the air guides of the climate control component.
3. If required, start a new check run.

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/note.png)Note:

The climate control component itself and its accessories for the air guidance, all the mechanical parts and all the part placements that are placed completely outside the enclosure are excluded from this check.

The climate control component and the part placements have to be assigned to an identical air-conditioning field (Property Power dissipation: Air-conditioning field (automatic) (ID 36046)).
