# Activating Quick Snap

The Quick snap feature will help you select symbols and macros quicker, and place them more reliably on the desired connections.

Precondition:

You have opened a project. You have opened an editor such as the graphical editor, the plot frame editor, etc.

1. Click the button ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/statusbar_logicsnap_as.png) (Quick snap on / off) in the status bar to activate the quick snap or deactivate it again.

### Select symbols by using Quick snap

1. Click the button ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/statusbar_logicsnap_as.png) (Quick snap on / off) in the status bar to activate the quick snap.
2. Hold down [Q], before you move the cursor over a symbol.   
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The insertion point of the symbol will be used as the snap point, meaning, the cursor will be strongly pulled to the insertion point. This is indicated by a small circle in the graphical editor.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Visualisation/ALL/gededitgui_pointsnap_as.png)

### Place symbols by using Quick snap

1. Click the button ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/statusbar_logicsnap_as.png) (Quick snap on / off) in the status bar to activate the quick snap.
2. First select the desired action (for example Insert center > Breadcrumb trail Home > Symbols), and then press the [Q] key.   
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) In this case, the activated Quick snap affects the connecting points of the symbols. The connecting points of a symbol then have the tendency to be pulled to an existing or possible connection line. This is indicated by a small double-cross in the graphical editor.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Visualisation/ALL/gededitgui_connectionsnap_as.png)
