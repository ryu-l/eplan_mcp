# Linking Segments with Pages

You have the option to link planning objects and structure segments with project pages. This means that you can see at the segment on which pages the corresponding data occur, for example the function, reports, etc. assigned to the planning object.

- Information of the segment can be displayed through a plot frame property by assigning a page to a segment. This means that the information which segment is placed on the page can, for example, be displayed automatically as a page description. If several segments are contained on the page, the information of the graphically first segment is displayed.
- If pages are assigned to a segment, these are also displayed in the graphical preview.
- During the reporting of segments the page names and descriptions of the assigned pages are output as well.
- The assignment of the pages to the copies of the segment is deleted during the copying of segments or the creation of pre-planning macros.

Preconditions:

- You have opened a project.
- You have created structure segments and planning objects.
- You have opened the pre-planning navigator and the page navigator.

1. Select a segment in the pre-planning navigator or in the graphical editor.
2. Select the Properties popup menu item and bring the Documents / Pages tab to the foreground.
3. Click ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/all_new_as.png) (New) above the Pages table.
4. Navigate through the displayed project pages and select the desired pages in the dialog Page selection.
5. Click [OK].  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The pages are entered in the list of assigned pages at the segment.
6. Select a line in the table and click ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/all_delete_as.png) (Delete) to remove the assignment of the corresponding page to the segment.
7. Click [OK].

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/info.png)Tip:

You can open a marked page in the graphical editor by using the popup menu items Open and Open in new window.
