# P026013: Part placement collides with restricted cut-out area

### Cause

A part placement on a mounting surface collides with a restricted cut-out area or vice versa.

Possible collisions:

- The part placement overlaps with the restricted cut-out area.
- The restricted cut-out area overlaps with the part placement.
- The part placement was positioned on the restricted cut-out area.
- The restricted cut-out area was positioned on the part placement.

### Solution

1. Locate the part placement or restricted cut-out area in the layout space using the Go to functionalities from the popup menu in the Message management dialog.
2. Either move the part placement or the restricted cut-out area on the mounting surface so that both objects do not collide with each other anymore.
3. If required, start a new check run.
