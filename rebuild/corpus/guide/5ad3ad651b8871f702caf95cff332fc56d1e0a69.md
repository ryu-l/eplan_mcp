# P502037: The standard or the company standard '<u>' in the macro '<x>' ('<y>' / '<z>') does not fit the standard or the company standard of the part property. The message applies to part property '<w>'

### Cause

In the parts database to be checked a part has been determined where the standard-specific part property '<w>' for the stored macro '<x>' does not fit the company standard or the standard which are saved in the macro. In the placeholder '<y>' of the message the representation type is output, in the placeholder '<z>' the variant of the macro.

### Solution

1. Match the standard and/or company standard saved in the macro to the standard or standard-specific property for the macro at the part. Or match the standard-specific property for the macro at the standard and/or the company standard saved in the macro.
2. If required, start a new check run.
