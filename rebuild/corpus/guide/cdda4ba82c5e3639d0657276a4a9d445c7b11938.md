# P005057: Net definition point with incorrect potential

### Cause

A net definition point was placed on a connection and equipped with a potential name. However, the potential is not defined at any place of the schematic.

### Solution

#### Possibility 1

Enter the name of an existing potential definition at the net definition point.

#### Possibility 2

1. In the schematic define a new potential, for example by means of a potential connection point.
2. Assign the potential name of the net definition point to this connection point.

---

  

If required, start a new check run.
