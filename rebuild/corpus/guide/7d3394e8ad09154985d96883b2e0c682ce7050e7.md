# P002002: Pin without targets

### Cause

#### Possibility 1

You placed a pin in the schematic without connecting it to a component.

#### Possibility 2

In the plugs navigator you have pre-defined a pin or a plug with several pins by means of the New or New functions popup menu item.

### Solution

#### Regarding option 1

1. Connect at least one of the symbol connection points of the placed pin to a component.
2. Update the connections.
3. If required, start a new check run.

#### Regarding option 2

Place the pin or the pins of the plug in the schematic and proceed for each pin as described in section "Regarding option 1".
