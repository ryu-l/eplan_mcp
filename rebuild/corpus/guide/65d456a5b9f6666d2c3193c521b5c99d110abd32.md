# P502020: The accessory part '<x>', variant '<y>', '<z>', entered at the part does not exist

### Cause

In the parts database to be checked a part at a main part was found that is entered in an accessory list, but does not exist as a record in the parts database. Eplan specifies the name of the part in the placeholder '<x>', the part variant in the placeholder '<y>' and the name of the accessory list in the placeholder '<z>'.

### Solution

1. Open the parts management and first create the required part. Or import the part.
2. Identify the part as an accessory part on the Accessories tab by activating the Part is accessory check box.
3. Click [OK].
4. Mark the desired accessory list in the tree view of the parts management.
5. Bring the Part tab to the foreground in the property table, and select the accessory part for the accessory list in the Part number field.
6. Close the parts management, save the modified data, and carry out a parts synchronization.
7. In the parts management mark the line with the message in the Messages tab and select the Go to popup menu item for this line.  
     
   ![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/arrow.png) The respective main part is marked in the tree view of the parts management.
8. Bring the Accessories tab to the foreground, and select the accessory list in the Part number / Name column in the property table.
9. Click [Apply].
10. If required, start a new check run.
11. Close the parts management and perform a parts data synchronization.
