# Form Functions: Alignment Boxes for Placeholders

Placeholders can be inserted into forms, which are replaced by text or graphics (e.g. jumpers) in the report tables.

Especially for terminal connection diagrams, different information can be graphically output:

- Any desired, but fixed graphic, e.g. for "screw symbols"
- Variable graphics per record, e.g. for special terminal types (PE / PEN)
- Generated graphic for cable lines, black boxes, and connection forks
- Symbol graphics.

It is sometimes necessary for the graphic to be scaled and for size limits to be pre-defined in the form. The graphic can also be oriented when it is placed in your alignment box. These settings are made under the Alignment box hierarchical level on the Format tab.

Two further settings are possible to align text, in addition to this orientation of the alignment box. These are also made on the Format tab, but in this case under the Text box hierarchical level.
