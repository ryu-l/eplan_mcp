# Division between on and off during an emergency stop

In order to make it possible for certain actuators to have their power cut off during an emergency-stop event, while other actuators, e.g. the gripper, continue to receive power, two bus types, on\_for\_emergency\_stop and off\_for\_emergency\_stop, are introduced. Depending on which outgoing bus is registered at a terminal, the connection will either continue to receive power in an emergency-off situation or not.
