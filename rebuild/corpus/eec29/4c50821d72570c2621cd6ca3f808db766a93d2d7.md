# Maximum Specification

A very common form of variant management is the working with maximum specifications of machines and plants. A project with maximum configuration serves as the template here. Components not needed are deleted and / or disabled in the specific project.

EEC supports this approach through the [Disabler](eecbase_k_variantmanagement_disabler.htm) concept. A disabler allows mechatronic and discipline-specific components to be disabled (not deleted). Individual components are disabled on the basis of parameter values, wit a disabler being either true or false.
