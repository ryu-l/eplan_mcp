### Constructor

Java

Constructors are special methods without result type whose name matches the name of the class. You therefore have to begin considering methods in the context in which they are needed or defined (are located). As the name suggests, constructors are used for "creating" (constructing) objects according to a certain model. Furthermore you can differentiate between implicit (specified) and explicit (self-created) constructors. Constructors perform the task of initializing the variables of the object, meaning of assigning a value to them. [Source:Wikipedia]
