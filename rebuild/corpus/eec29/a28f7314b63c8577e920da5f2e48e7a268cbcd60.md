# Inserting functions as called POUs in OB_Cycle

![](../Pictures/Gui/ALL/note.png)Note:

POUs, or program organizational units, are used to structure programs. POUs of the type FB ([Function](glossary_o_fc.htm) Block) and FC (Function) are used here.

The functions FC\_ASI\_CopyInputs and FC\_Org\_Functions are to be called as POUs of OB\_Cycle.

1. [Double](glossary_o_double.htm)-click to open the OB\_Cycle block.
2. Select the Called POUs editor.
3. Open the finder for POUs by clicking ![](../Pictures/button_finder.png).
4. Click [Search].
5. Select the functions FC\_ASI\_CopyInputs and FC\_Org\_Functions.
6. Confirm with [OK].

The functions are subsequently entered in the Called POUs editor.
