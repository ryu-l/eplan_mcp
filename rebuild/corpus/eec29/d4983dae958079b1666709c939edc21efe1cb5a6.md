# Installing the fragments FR_Error_Dec and FR_Error_NW in Axis and Separator Function units

The fragments FR\_Error\_Dec and FR\_Error\_NW must now be installed and configured in the Axis and Separator [Function](glossary_o_fc.htm) units.

1. Open the Axis Function unit.
2. Drag the fragments FR\_Error\_Dec and FR\_Error\_NW from the Step7 unit into the component editor.
3. Repeat steps 1 and 2 for the Separator Function unit.
