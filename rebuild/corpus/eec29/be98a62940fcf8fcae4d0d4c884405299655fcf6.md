### Placeholder object

Electrical engineering

Multiple objects can be assigned to one placeholder object. The properties of these objects are assigned to variables. A placeholder object is indicated in EPLAN by a specific 'anchor' symbol and located on the separate level EPLAN322, "Symbol gaphic.Macro.Placeholder objects".
