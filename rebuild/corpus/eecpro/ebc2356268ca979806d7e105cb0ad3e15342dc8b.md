# Inserting and configuring the Caller parameter in FR_SymbolTable and FR_SymbolsGeneral

The fragments FR\_SymbolTable and FR\_SymbolsGeneral also require the parameter Caller to be integrated in the discipline structure by means of the Plug-Socket principle.

Inserting the Caller parameter:

1. Open the fragment FR\_SymbolTable.
2. Select the Parameters editor.
3. Drag the Caller parameter from the Parameter unit into the editor.
4. For the Caller parameter, enter the value SymbolTable.
5. Repeat steps 1 to 4 for the fragment FR\_SymbolsGeneral.
