# Installing the S7_313C ControllerComponent in Feeder Station

Previously the SiemensCPU controller was installed directly in the Feeder station (see [Adding discipline components to mechatronic components](tutstep7_h_task1_insert_dc1.htm)). Instead of the SiemensCPU controller, now the S7\_313C mechatronic component is installed in the Feeder station. The SiemensCPU controller is first uninstalled.

Uninstalling the SiemensCPU controller:

1. Open the Feeder station.
2. Select the SiemensCPU controller in the component editor.
3. Click on the Delete component symbol or press the [Del] button.

Installing the S7\_313C component in the Feeder station

1. Open the Feeder station.
2. Drag the S7\_313C component into the component editor.
