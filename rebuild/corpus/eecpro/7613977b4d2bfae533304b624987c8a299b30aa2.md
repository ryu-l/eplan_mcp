# Shortcuts

By means of the ampersand (&amp; or &#38;) **shortcuts** are specified, to ensure the usability of the Form-UI in absence of the mouse. Shortcuts are **underlined characters** of a word, which is displayed in a menu or a button. The combination of the [Alt] key and the underlined letter will trigger the desired function, according to a mouse clickon the menu item or button.

In addition a button should be used by the shortcut [Alt] + [S]:

### [ClosedExample](javascript:void(0);)

```
<open type="button" receiver="mos('Station')" ref="doku" >Go to &amp;Station</open>
```
