# Connection via T-pieces or brackets

Besides the basic wiring diagram and the lamp, the entire circuit also needs the correct connections for each position. All lamps â except for the last one â require T-pieces for correct wiring. The last lamp is connected via a bracket. Since the lamp requires two connections in this representation form, the window macro must also have two connections. The macro does not contain any parameters, which is why no placeholder is needed in this case. The Autoconnecting function connects the two opposing brackets or T-pieces with each other automatically, but this is not an error, and is supplemented by the graphic of the lamp after the assembly during the generation process.

The positioning of the graphic can be realized only by means of a plug.
