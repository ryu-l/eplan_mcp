# Placing asymmetrical devices on a mounting rail

By default, devices are positioned with mounting point M4 in the middle of the mounting rail. If the position of the mounting rail mounting bracket deviates from the center of the device (asymmetrical devices), the 3D graphical macro has its own handle G1.

![](../Pictures/2014011701.svg)

Enter your own handle G1 instead of mounting point M4 in parameter Mate:

```
=List{'G1','M2',0}
```
