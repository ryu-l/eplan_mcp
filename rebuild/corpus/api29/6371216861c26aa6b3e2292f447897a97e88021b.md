Remarks

Representation type of macro. Possible values are:

1 = Multi-line

2 = Single-line

3 = Pair cross-reference

4 = Overview

5 = Graphic

6 = Panel layout

7 = P&I diagram

8 = Multi-line Fluid power

9 = Topology

10 = 3D mounting layout

11 = Functional

12 = Pre-planning.
