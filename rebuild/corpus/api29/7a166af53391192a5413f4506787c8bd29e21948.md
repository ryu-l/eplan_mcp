#### Parameters

*dNewLength*
:   Length.

*nFixBendingPoint*
:   Fix bending point (0,...,n)

*nMovingBendingPoint*
:   Moving bending point (Must be greater or less than fix bending point)

Remarks

A bus bar with one angle has three bending points (0,1,2).
