# RemoveStaticMenuItem Method

RemoveStaticMenuItem Method

Removes a static menu item (which was added with AddStatic(Popup)MenuItem. When it was a popup, the complete popup menu is removed, including its separators The menu is also removed from settings

- [C++/CLI](#i-syntax-CPP2005)
