#### Parameters

*plcmnts*
:   Placements to move.

*oPntStart*
:   Starting point of a line(mirror)

*oPntEnd*
:   Ending point of a line(mirror)

| Exception | Description |
| --- | --- |

| [System.ArgumentException](#) | Is thrown in case of oPntStart is equal to oPntEnd |
