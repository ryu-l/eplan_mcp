Remarks

Calculated in relation to origin of LayoutSpace coordinate system. For more information see the chapter: [API\_Pro\_Panel](API_Pro_Panel.html). Currently scaling 3d objects in P8 is not supported, therefore setting cell `M33` to value different then `1` may cause incorrect result.
