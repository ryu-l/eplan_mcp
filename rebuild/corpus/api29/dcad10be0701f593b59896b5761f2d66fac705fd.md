#### Property Value

Remarks

Determines the technology to be applied or selected. The following values are possible:

0 = Electrical engineering,

1 = Mechanics,

2 = Hydraulics,

3 = Pneumatics,

4 = Cooling,

5 = Lubrication,

6 = Process engineering,

7 = Cooling lubricant,

8 = Gas engineering,

9 = Fluid power, general.
