# DoReassignWires Method

DoReassignWires Method

All wires in cable are assigned to a matching template of the cable or shielding. User specifies templates to which wires will be assigned. Works only for [Eplan.EplApi.DataModel.EObjects.Cable](Eplan.EplApi.DataModelu~Eplan.EplApi.DataModel.EObjects.Cable.html) or [Eplan.EplApi.DataModel.Function](Eplan.EplApi.DataModelu~Eplan.EplApi.DataModel.Function.html) with category [Eplan.EplApi.DataModel.Function.Enums.Category.Shielding](Eplan.EplApi.DataModelu~Eplan.EplApi.DataModel.Function+Enums+Category.html).
