    StyleFactorLine Style Factor when positive or Pattern Length when negative. Default value = 1. Please use "-16002" as "from layer" value.

    0 = continous: ------

    1 = dash: - - - - -

    2 = dot: .......

    3 = dashdot: _._._._

    4 = dashdotdot: _.._.._

    5 = dash: _ _

    6 = dashlongdot: __ _
