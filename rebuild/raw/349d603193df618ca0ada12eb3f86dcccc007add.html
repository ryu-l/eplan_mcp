Eplan Actions: Overview

The Actions listed below are available to you in Eplan for different fields of application. An "X" in a column identifies the field of application recommended by us for the respective action. The actions identified with "X*" in the Ribbon column are available to you in the dialog Customize for customizing the ribbon as pre-defined commands.

Further information on the actions is available in the Eplan API help of the Platform. A list of all official Eplan actions is available in the section Actions.
